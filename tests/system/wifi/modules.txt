# modules.txt: the modules of linux-lts that the Linux AP guest loads at
# boot, each with what it needs before it, in the order modules.dep gives.
# mac80211_hwsim, last, is loaded by wifiap with radios=4.
#
# e1000 drives QEMU's cards; af_packet carries the EAPOL and DHCP frames of
# hostapd, wpa_supplicant and dnsmasq; bridge joins network A's two access
# points; nlmon captures the netlink of the trace. The kernel asks for the
# ciphers mac80211 uses by name, and finds them registered: ccm and ctr for
# CCMP, cmac for BIP, gcm and ghash-generic for the GCMP suites, libarc4 for
# TKIP's RC4. The netfilter modules are those iptables' NAT rules need,
# through nf_tables and its compatibility with the xtables targets.
