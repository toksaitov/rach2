# Step 6: Venus on the host's lavapipe with the udmabuf module

Row 6 of `run2-plan.md`. It decides the plan's decision A, the agents' way to test Rach2's virtio-gpu 3D, and deliverable 2's transport.

* Build `udmabuf` as a module for the running WSL kernel, 6.18.54.1-microsoft-standard-WSL2: Microsoft's source for exactly that version from its release tarball, configured from the running kernel's config, built once in `/home/testing/rach2/build/run2/step6/` for `Module.symvers`, then the module with the small local patch it needs (`CONFIG_UDMABUF` is a bool, the kernel exports no `vmap_pfn`; `memfd_fcntl` may need one too). The first try, in `tonight/` notes and `dev-log/76-graphics/research/work/notes.md`, stopped before the download finished. Keep the patch as a diff and the module under `build/cache/run2/`.
* Load it as root only with no other job in WSL: check that no `tests/run`, QEMU, compiler or loop of any account runs, and that the ledger is empty. A fault there can take WSL down for every account; if it does, say so at once. Give `/dev/udmabuf` to the `kvm` group for the run only, by hand, not by a rule that persists. Do not set it to load at boot.
* Then Venus on the host's lavapipe: QEMU 10.2.1 with KVM, `virtio-gpu-gl,venus=on,blob=on,hostmem=...`, a Debian 13 guest at level 2: `vulkaninfo` (version, swapchain, external memory), vkcube with a picture (QEMU's display, read back by QMP's `screendump` or the guest's own capture), DXVK and vkd3d-proton programs from step 2, Chromium's WebGPU on Venus. Compare with the first run's Debian-VM host at level 2 with a guest under TCG at level 3.
* Unload the module at the end and say what is left on the host. Close with decision A's answer.
