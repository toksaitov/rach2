# Graphics: the discussions and research before the plan

The discussions, research and plan drafts for graphics on Rach2, from the owner's first talk with Codex on 2026-10-06 to the approved design of the second discovery run on 2026-11-06. Milestone 76's plan starts from here. Nothing in this directory is edited after the fact; the files are kept as they were written, and the decisions in them are the owner's unless a file says otherwise.

Read in this order:

* `decisions.md`: the owner's decisions and the project's direction as the chat recorded them, with the facts about the machines that the plan rests on.
* `work/plan.md`: draft 5 of the plan, the last one, corrected by the measurements of the night of 2026-11-04. `work/plan-v1.md` to `plan-v4.md` are the earlier drafts.
* `work/run2-plan.md`: the second discovery run, approved on 2026-11-06 and not yet run.
* `work/notes.md`: the chat's working log of the research, with where each piece came from.
* `codex-thread.md`: the owner's thread "rach2-graphics" with Codex, the first discussion of the stack.

The rest of `work/` is the work behind the plan:

* `brief.md`, `findings-chat.md`, `position-chat.md`, `codex/`, `fable.md`, `comparison.md`, `fable-round2.md`, `round3/` and `second/`: the research brief, the chat's own findings and position, the reports of Codex, Fable, Argon (agy) and Muse Wonder Full, and the rounds in which they argued.
* `tonight/`: the measurements on the Lenovo and in QEMU on the testing PC on the night of 2026-11-04, and Codex's and Fable's reviews of the plan in the light of them.
* `breakdown/`: the drafts that broke the plan into milestones.
* `env/`: the study of the best graphics test environment for agents on the testing PC.

Paths inside these files name `build/flow/coordinator/graphics-research/`, where they were written. The raw outputs, logs and scripts of those runs are not here; they stay in that folder on the Mac, and the whole folder as it stood on 2026-11-08 is `build/flow/coordinator/archive/graphics-research-20261108.tar.gz`.
