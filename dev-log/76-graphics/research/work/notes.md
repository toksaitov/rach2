* Wayland with Weston kiosk-shell; full Chromium and Firefox.

* 32-bit programs on the 64-bit kernel is a must (Windows Office on Wine, old games).
* Proton with WineD3D (OpenGL) on the Lenovo; Vulkan path later on better hardware.
* Research direction later: Linux driver VM as a GPU service; design the native stack's contracts so it plugs in.
