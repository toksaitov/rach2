# 76-38: the kernel without its symbols

Task 76-38: the kernel the loaders boot keeps no symbols but `_start` and `_end`, and `kernel.debug` keeps them all, to make room in the 4 MiB kernel slot of a boot partition until the larger slot comes at the stack's end. The x86-64 kernel grew some 14 KB an hour as the stack's tasks merged and stood 91,320 bytes under the slot.

## Sizes

Measured on 2026-11-11 on the testing host, on milestone/76-display at 9fdd0982e, before and after the change to `rach2/Makefile`'s link. Before, `objcopy --strip-debug --discard-all` made `kernel`; after, `objcopy --strip-all --keep-symbol=_start --keep-symbol=_end` makes it.

| Kernel | Before | After | Saved | Under the slot after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| i686 `build/rach2/kernel` | 3,350,536 | 3,181,416 | 169,120 | 1,012,888 |
| x86-64 `build/x86_64/rach2/kernel` | 4,102,984 | 3,871,208 | 231,776 | 323,096 |
| x86-64 without the i386 ABI, `rach2-pure64/kernel` | 3,965,392 | 3,740,136 | 225,256 | 454,168 |

The loaded sections do not change: `size` gives text 2,878,341 and data 268,636 for i686, and text 3,592,142 and data 274,152 for x86-64, before and after. For each kernel, `kernel` was compared with the old strip of the same `kernel.debug`: the program headers and the entry are the same, and the files differ only in the ELF header's `e_shoff` and in the section headers and symbol tables after the last load's file bytes.

## Readers of the symbols

No loader reads the symbol table, which lies in no loaded segment. GRUB and QEMU's `-kernel` load the i686 kernel by its program headers, and the x86-64 kernel by the a.out kludge of its multiboot header, from file bytes that are unchanged. The kernel built on Rach2 by TinyCC and pdpmake (`rach2/guest.mk`) has no symbol table and is not made by objcopy. No kernel source reads its own symbols.

Three readers looked up symbols in `kernel`:

* `tools/inspect/elfcheck --kernel`, run on every link, compares `_start` with the entry and `_end` with the image end; those two are kept.
* host.host.pure64 (`tests/cases/host/pure64check.c`) looks for nine functions of the i386 ABI in both x86-64 kernels. It reads `kernel.debug` now, which its prepare list names, since a release's preparation ships only the files a case names; the root Makefile and `tests/system/suites.mk` gained rules for the two `kernel.debug` files, which link the kernel again when its `kernel.debug` is gone.
* `tests/system/build_inputs_test.sh` reads its linker-script marker from `kernel.debug` now, and checks that `kernel` keeps `_start` and `_end` alone.
