# Reflections

## 2026-11-03 18:49

After the joint release of milestones 67 to 70 and the sitting of milestone 71, at about 591,000 lines of in-house C and 8,417 commits, the owner was asked what was thought of the project and answered:

> [...]
>
> The development log is very important, and its size is not a problem. As I see it, a record of building an OS that can run most popular software, all within a year, is a treasure in its own right. We can use it to publish a paper on our experience and findings, give talks, or at least write blog posts. It is a shame that I dropped the system that recorded all our conversations and kept them versioned on GitHub, since we would have even more material now. Oh well. Don't bring it back; just make sure we keep maintaining the dev log properly.
>
> By the way, whenever I take one of these philosophical pauses and write long posts about the project or its direction, we should probably record them in the dev log too, as part of the project's history.
>
> Ask though first, when you want to make my musings part of the development log.
