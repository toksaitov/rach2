1. Both goals are feasible, but several tasks cannot establish their stated result.

   * 68-5 names `audio_start` and `audio_stop` wrappers that do not exist. Console ioctls call the hardware driver directly at [syscall.c:2118](rach2/sys/syscall.c:2118). Changing `audio.c` alone requires an explicit dispatch driver between `audio` and the hardware. Define that design in 2a, including separate playback and capture operations, buffer ownership and wakeups. The present [driver interface](rach2/sys/audio/audio.h:39) describes playback alone.
   * 67-12a needs more than new `prctl` cases. Capability sets are currently all-or-none in the initial namespace, and exec can restore privilege: [syscall_proc.c:7441](rach2/sys/syscall_proc.c:7441), [syscall_proc.c:1456](rach2/sys/syscall_proc.c:1456). Assign bounding-set inheritance, exec enforcement and credential handling explicitly. Test that a dropped capability cannot return through fork and exec. Include each agent's actual sandbox invocation, especially Codex's bundled bubblewrap.
   * Several Dones prove operation but not the promised improvement. Four CPUs running work does not prove bounded scheduler searches; [sched_find and sched_steal](rach2/sys/proc/sched.c:112) currently walk the process list. Count selection work with many blocked threads. Likewise, a bounded reported audio delay does not prove that stale samples were discarded. Add distinguishable samples before and after a stall and check their order and playback position, including after virtual-channel mixing lands.
   * 67-21b has not established a soft, audible bell on the Lenovo. The [PC speaker implementation](rach2/sys/arch/x86/speaker.c:25) controls pitch and gating, with no volume control. Measure that path before relying on it. Kernel speaker counts cannot prove audibility or softness; the planned listening step remains necessary.

2. The dependency and ownership tables need these corrections.

   * 67-18a cannot finish its user-facing IPv6 proof within its ownership. [socket_kind](rach2/sys/syscall_net.c:384) rejects AF_INET6 and packet protocol 0x86DD, while 18c owns `syscall_net.c`. The current [ping interface](utils/pingcall.h:20) also accepts only a 32-bit IPv4 destination. Move the address type and minimal IPv6 raw/socket entry points into an initial interface task, or give 18a an internal packet proof and move `ping -6` later. Adding an 18c dependency to 18a would create a cycle. Also, 18c's off-host link-local proof needs 18b's neighbour resolution; keep its independent Done to loopback or add that wait.
   * 68-1a's extraction does not protect milestone 67. These are separate branches, and 67 releases first. Its [hda_hosttest.c:56](tests/host/hda_hosttest.c:56) will still include all of `procfs.c`. Give 67 responsibility for keeping that test buildable, or arrange an explicit shared prerequisite commit. A task closing on 68 does not deliver its code to 67.
   * 67-9 is much wider than its file list. Descriptor tables expose a contiguous [entries array](rach2/sys/fs/fd.h:188), directly indexed throughout syscall, procfs, namespace, device and IPC code. For example, [procfs.c:4798](rach2/sys/fs/procfs.c:4798) bypasses `fd_get`. Chunked storage needs an accessor conversion with assigned ownership, or a specified way to preserve contiguous virtual addresses. Establish that interface before concurrent descriptor consumers, including 68's new devices.
   * Missing audio waits: 1c's `/proc/audio` proof consumes 1a; 2d's HDA and SB16 proof consumes 2b and 2c; 6a and 6b consume task 9's fixtures; 7's playback and recording Dones consume 2b and 3b. Keep port builds early by moving their device proofs to an integration task, rather than making a worker hold the task while waiting. The ADC proofs need either 2e's launcher change or an explicitly independent boot command; the current [launcher selects `hda-output`](tools/etude/qemu_profile.c:858).
   * Some shared functions still have two owners. 67-7a and 9 both change [alloc_process](rach2/sys/proc/process.c:106); 8 and 17 both change the [TCP timeout constants](rach2/sys/network/tcp/tcp.h:68); 4b's permission-sensitive cache and 10's notifications meet in [vfs_setattr](rach2/sys/fs/vfs.c:1785). Give each a defined handoff. Explicitly reserve topology's procfs/sysfs functions for 11b instead of leaving them under 11a's "rest." On audio, order 4's start/stop changes after 2b, or separate their functions before either starts.
   * The two libtsm patches can remain parallel with the stated regeneration rule. Shared manifests, source lists and tables still require reconciliation on the final branch. Require both libtsm patches to apply together with the build's existing `-F 0`; separate successful builds do not establish that.

3. There are missing implementation, licensing and machine steps.

   * Task 22 introduces a concrete license conflict. [Doom's SHA-1 source](ports/doom/src/sha1.c:8) is GPL-3.0-or-later. Its [Makefile](ports/doom/Makefile:48) links that source with the in-house platform and sound files. Making those files GPL-2.0-only leaves incompatible terms for the combined executable; the syscall note does not cover this linkage. Replace SHA-1 with a compatible implementation, or obtain a specific licensing decision for the affected port. [GNU's compatibility explanation](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#v2v3Compatibility) confirms the conflict. Task 22 must also update [Doom's license section](ports/doom/Readme.md:34), preserve upstream notices and supply the applicable license texts.
   * FFmpeg's feature list omits parts of the measured working configuration: PCM encoders, file/pipe protocols and audio filters. They are present in [the build record](build/tmp/soxmeasure/notes.txt:35). Restore them explicitly. Require full conversion of every fixture to checked PCM output; merely opening an input or listing decoders is insufficient. The LGPL choice is sound with the stated exclusions, but retain notices and verify the built configuration and license. [FFmpeg's licensing guidance](https://www.ffmpeg.org/legal.html) supports that approach.
   * Pin provenance needs an explicit deliverable for cmus, bubblewrap and socat. [sources.txt](vendor/gnu/sources.txt:64) documents both signature verification and alternatives for unsigned releases. Follow those precedents. For sox_ng, record the exact chosen release, URL, digest and absence of a signature. Its separate musl-vendor SHA-256 pin is not itself a violation of the GNU source rules.
   * 68-2d must own Doom's header/build integration. [Doom's Makefile](ports/doom/Makefile:18) uses private headers and does not search musl's installed headers. Installing `<sys/soundcard.h>` in musl alone will not make the proposed include compile.
   * 68-2b needs a program that configures the same open descriptor before passing it to `cat`. Raw `cat` cannot negotiate stereo, rate or sample format. State the OSS defaults and test the negotiated format.
   * The Lenovo rollback procedure is incomplete for tasks that install new userland. [rach2-install:1050](utils/rach2-install:1050) states that both kernel slots boot the same root. Restoring both kernels leaves a trial root installed. Specify deployment and restoration of the root and recovery files as well. This is especially important for 67-5: the old kernel mounts the proposed extra-isize filesystem read-only at [ext2.c:5528](rach2/sys/fs/ext2.c:5528).
   * Broaden 67-8's inventory before estimating it. Storage deadlines also assume 100 Hz: [request.c:134](rach2/sys/block/request.c:134) and [msc.c:159](rach2/sys/usb/msc.c:159). The 64-bit conversion reaches process deadlines, wait queues and clock APIs beyond the listed files.
   * Name the affected contract documents: process/scheduler, fd/VFS, namespace and memory Readmes; syscall pages for tasks 9, 12a, 14, 15, 16 and 19; and `rach2-install(8)` and recovery documentation for 20. Move task 22's prosecheck and the host-test pass requirements to the end checks, consistently with the prompt's prohibition on checks during the milestone.

The small isolated FFmpeg build does not establish the port tasks' elapsed time.

Separate state directories would still share build copies, including Windows' tree. Ledger admission does not solve that conflict.

Recalculate the overall range after fixing the dependency graph and measuring GNU rebuilds; 15 to 22 hours is presently optimistic.
