# The GPU to a Linux VM on the Lenovo (82-4b)

On the Lenovo, Rach2's Intel driver gives the HD 4400 to Debian's QEMU on a trial root of the x86-64 GNU root, which gives it through vfio-pci to step 11's Debian guest of the second discovery run; the guest's i915 lights the panel and runs glmark2 under Weston, and Rach2 takes the GPU back after a clean power-off and after QEMU is killed under load, with the pipe's CRC and a GL readback on Crocus checked each time. This file has the guest as the step runs it, Linux's reference (82-4b.2) and the readiness note the trial waits on (82-4b.1); the step's commands are on the task's issue, and its results come here when it has run.

## The guest

The guest is step 11's (`research/run2/step11.md` in the graphics milestone's directory): Debian 13 with its kernel 6.12.115+deb13.1, Mesa 25.0.7-2+deb13u1, Weston 14.0.2 and glmark2 2023.01+dfsg-2, the disk an overlay on step 10's guest, 24 GiB virtual, with `snd_hda_intel`, `snd_hda_codec_hdmi` and `snd_hda_codec` blacklisted, on the Lenovo's p5 under `/srv/run2/`. Rach2 cannot read p5, whose ext4 has extents, so a Debian boot of the step runs [`82-4/prep.sh`](82-4/prep.sh), which copies the guest's disk to the data partition as one qcow2 image without a backing file, with the guest's kernel, initrd and NoCloud seed, and installs in the copy [`82-4/g82.sh`](82-4/g82.sh), its unit [`82-4/g82.service`](82-4/g82.service) and step 9's `weston.ini` ([`82-4/weston.ini`](82-4/weston.ini)). Step 11's files are only read.

The unit runs only on a boot whose command line has `g82=MODE`, and writes every line to the serial console, which `tests/assign/gpu.sh` keeps:

* `g82=probe` prints the function at 00:02.0, i915's lines of the log, the display's state from debugfs, the connectors and the backlight, then powers off;
* `g82=run` adds Weston on the GPU, through the guest's seatd, and glmark2's default set fullscreen, GL then GLES, with the GPU's clock read each second, then powers off;
* `g82=hold` starts the same Weston and glmark2's terrain scene with `--run-forever`, and prints `G82 LOAD N` each second it runs, for `gpu.sh -K` to kill QEMU under load.

`gpu.sh` starts QEMU with the q35 command line of step 11 and 82-4a: `-machine q35,kernel-irqchip=split -accel kvm -cpu host -m 3072 -smp 4 -nodefaults -vga none -display none -device vfio-pci,host=0000:00:02.0,bus=pcie.0,addr=02.0,x-igd-opregion=on`, SeaBIOS, the guest's kernel and initrd loaded by QEMU, and, as step 11's `vm.sh` gave them, the disk and the seed on virtio, a user-mode virtio NIC, virtio's RNG, keyboard and tablet, no sound device, and SeaBIOS's log on the debug port 0x402. The disk is opened with `snapshot=on`, so that every run starts from the same image.

[`82-4/check.sh`](82-4/check.sh) reads Rach2's side between the runs: the function's driver and nodes, the pipe's CRC of 78-10d's pattern at the panel's own mode by `crcmodes`, `eglinfo` naming Crocus, piglit's `gl-1.0-readpixsanity` and `fbo-readpixels` on the GPU's render node, and the counts of IOMMU faults, VFIO, KVM and free vectors.

## Readiness (82-4b.1)

Why no path the trial adds can leave the Lenovo stopped, as the `machine` skill's Trial boots asks. Line numbers are those of `rach2/sys` at this file's commit.

### What has run on the Lenovo, and what has not

Branch kernels have run there with VT-d programmed and interrupt remapping on (81-6d.3, and every trial root booted with the release's line, 78-10d's among them), the Intel driver bound with its display, setting modes and reading the pipe's CRC (78-10d), the GPU reset after a hang (78-6c), and Debian guests of the GNU root's QEMU under VMX (81-3b). What this trial runs there for the first time:

1. The driver's release (`handoff_release`, `i915_handoff.c:294`): the display off by one commit, the rings stopped and the GPU reset (`i915_gt_quiesce`, `i915_reset.c:280`), the DRM device unplugged, the register windows unmapped, bus mastering off and the kernel's claim given up.
2. vfio-pci on the GPU: the function-level reset at the open (`vfio_device_enable`, `vfio/pci.c:149`), by the Advanced Features capability, the GPU's only reset method (`reset_method` `af_flr pm` under Linux); the OpRegion read from ACPI NVS; the BARs mapped into QEMU as device memory; the GPU's message through a remapped entry of dmar0 into an eventfd.
3. Translation on dmar0 (`attach_locked`, `vtd.c:884`): dmar0 has no pass-through and no large pages (ecap 0xf0101a, cap 0xc0000020660462), so the attach first builds its identity domain (`identity_new`, `vtd.c:543`) in 4 KiB pages below the end of RAM, then the guest's domain of 3 GiB of pinned pages, every table written back from the cache since dmar0 does not walk coherently, the GPU's context entry, and TE.
4. KVM with a device assigned: the VFIO bridge makes the VM non-coherent (`kvm/vfio.c:78`), so EPT takes the guest's PAT for memory, and device pages are uncached in EPT (`tdp.c:115`), as Linux's `vmx_get_mt_mask`.
5. The end of QEMU, by power-off or SIGKILL: the device's release resets the function before its group leaves the container (`device_put`, `vfio/pci.c:62`; `vfio_device_release`, 194), so the GPU stops while it still translates through the guest's domain; then the detach puts the host's context entry back and turns TE off (`iommu_detach`, `vtd.c:975`), and the pins go back.
6. The bring-up (`handoff_bring_up`, `i915_handoff.c:406`): the function reset again, the panel's power cycle waited out, and the driver's probe with nothing to adopt, then the console's mode set from a dark panel: the panel's power on, the AUX channel and link training. 78-10d set modes only on a panel already lit.

### Register sequences

* The PCI reset follows Linux 6.12's `drivers/pci/pci.c` line by line: `pci_dev_save_and_disable` (`pci_reset_function`, `pci.c:1523`), `pci_af_flr` with `pci_wait_for_pending` on the status byte's TP, the write of the FLR bit and 100 ms (`reset_by`, 1481), `pci_dev_wait` with its waits doubling from 1 ms (`wait_ready`, 1454), whose sum after nine waits is the 1023 ms Linux logged for this GPU in step 11 ("ready 1023ms after AF_FLR"), and `pci_dev_restore`.
* The release's display commit is the disable half of the mode set 78-10d.1 checked against `hsw_crtc_disable` and `intel_ddi_post_disable_dp`; the GT's stop and reset are 78-6c's, checked against `intel_gt_reset`'s gen6 path.
* TE is set and cleared as Linux's `iommu_enable_translation` and `iommu_disable_translation` do, from the persistent command bits kept in the driver, never from the status read back (`vtd_set_gcmd`, `vtd.c:114`); the context entry is written, written back and invalidated, context cache then IOTLB, before TE, as 82-1.2's host rows count against the specification's order.

### Every wait is bounded

* The release's commit: a vblank at 100 ms, the panel's sequencer at 5 s, the DDI buffer's idle state, as 78-10d.1 lists them; the GPU's reset: GDRST read for 2 ms at a time, the reset tried four times with 10, 20 and 30 ms between (`i915_reset.c:268`); a GPU that will not reset refuses the release with the driver bound and wedged.
* The PCI reset: the pending transactions read at 0, 100, 200 and 400 ms, then reset anyway with a warning, as Linux; the function's return waited for at most 60 s (`PCI_READY_MS`, `pci.c:1347`, Linux's `PCIE_RESET_READY_POLL_MS`), then `-ETIMEDOUT`, which leaves the function without a driver and SSH up.
* VT-d: each command and queued invalidation waits 1 s at most and logs `no answer` (`wait_status`, `vtd.c:92`; `qi_submit`, 176). Linux waits 10 s there and panics.
* The bring-up: the power cycle is one sleep of the VBT's delay, 510 ms at least; the probe's waits are the bind's, each with its bound and its log line (forcewake 50 ms, a ring's stop 5 ms, GDRST); the mode set's are 78-10d.1's, the sequencer's power on at 5 s and AUX at 10 ms with five sends.
* `gpu.sh` stops QEMU after 600 s with no byte on the guest's console, and gives the function back to the driver after any failure once it has left.

### Interrupts and faults

* The GPU's message under vfio-pci goes through an entry of dmar0's remapping table with the GPU's source id, set when QEMU enables it and cleared before the reset at the release (`vfio_irq_disable`); the host's own vectors are untouched, and a message from the guest's device that names another entry is blocked and counted, as 82-3.4 forced in QEMU.
* A DMA fault of the GPU while it translates through the guest's domain is recorded by dmar0, which serves the GPU alone, logged up to 32 times and counted (`fault_one`, `fault.c:67`); the host's devices are on dmar1, which never translates in this trial, so their DMA is not affected. The step's pass needs no fault counted.

### The host test and the runs in QEMU

* `tests/host/i915_handoff_hosttest.c` runs this release and bring-up whole on the model of the HD 4400, through the two files of /sys, twice, with the reset clearing what step 11's run of Linux showed cleared, to a full mode set of the panel with the link trained and ten frames counted, and no wait at its bound (82-4a.1). It passed alone under ASan and UBSan on the Mac at dfe83270b, the head of `milestone/76-display` from which this task was cut, with `i915-handoff-test: ok`. Since 82-4a recorded its run, the handoff's own file has changed only in how `/sys` shows the bound driver (36fc29ad2); the driver around it has changed too, among others in the flip's wait for a framebuffer's pending writes and the remapped views of wide framebuffers, and the run at dfe83270b includes them.
* 82-4a's four cases ran `gpu.sh` whole in QEMU, under SeaBIOS and under UEFI, with a clean power-off and with SIGKILL, on EDU under the fake chip, and every count of VFIO, KVM and free vectors came back; 82-3's cases ran VFIO through an emulated VT-d with remapped messages and forced DMA and interrupt faults.

### What neither can vouch for, and how the step meets it

* The chip after its own reset under Rach2's probe, and the panel's power on from dark. A fault there leaves the panel dark or the probe refused with SSH up; the console and the step's checks show it, and the put-back reboot gives the firmware's picture back.
* dmar0's tables walked by the real unit. A wrong table makes the GPU's DMA fault, which is counted, while the host goes on.
* The first entry of a guest with a device assigned under VMX. The step therefore runs the whole path first with `g82=probe`, about a minute of guest, reads the host's log over SSH, and only then the glmark2 run, the kill and the UEFI probe, each followed by `check.sh` and the log.
* The trial kernel is the milestone branch's head with this task's commits, which add no kernel code. It carries 60 kernel commits made since the last branch kernel booted on the Lenovo (9fdd0982e, 81-3b's of 2026-11-11), among them the ATA and lag clocks' tick counts, TCP's zero-window probes, pipe pages, PTY locking and the vDSO page refused to ptrace; they run at the trial root's boot, before any of the paths above, and the step stops on any warning or failed probe that the release's log lacks.
