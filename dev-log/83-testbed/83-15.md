# 83-15: Linux's figures on the Lenovo for the release's Lenovo boot

Task 83-15 (#4655) takes, in Debian on the Lenovo, the Linux references that three of the end's runs lack and that a Rach2 boot cannot make, and leaves on the data partition the files the release's native cases read: 78-13b's IGT buffer rows, glmark2 validations, WebGL pages and the gate's probe lines; 78-14b's video output with its clips; 83-7's `native-linux` scenes and the bed's VM disks. The manager added one 0 A.D. replay to settle Linux's reference for 84's frame Done. The release's Lenovo boot (83-13, #4648) judges Rach2 against them.

## The step

Two boots of Debian 13.7 on p5 by 80-2's route (`tools/lenovo/debian-ref/`, `mac.sh arm`, `wait-debian`, `leave`, `wait-rach2`, `restore`), under the Lenovo's lock, with the notice posted on #4655 at 16:22. Everything was built and staged first: IGT's stage, the bed's VM image and the forward guest on the testing host, `build/t4655-igt.tar.gz` and the disks fetched to the Mac.

1. On the release, bb151e3ba, booted from the candidate and the same kernel in the fallback (`machine_identity.sh`): the data partition `/dev/disk0p6` mounted, `native/video/clips` and `native/bed` written from the Mac (the VM's raw disk made with `qemu-img convert -O raw` and sent sparse through `zstd`), each file checked against `tests/graphics/ref/lenovo-video/data.sha256` and `tests/kvm/ref/lenovo-bed.sha256`, owned by root, and unmounted.
2. The first boot, with `intel_iommu=on` in a local copy of `boot.cfg` in place of `intel_iommu=off`, as the release boots Rach2 with VT-d at its default: `conformance.sh`'s parts igt, glmark2, gate, probe and webgl, then `collect`; `measure.sh -r native-linux -u gfx` for glmark2, piglit, game and webgl; `games.sh 0ad` twice, with the GPU's minimum clock at 1000 MHz as 84-25's `bkl.sh` pins Rach2's, then free, each with Mesa's shader cache emptied. SuperTuxKart and 0 A.D. were installed for the boot on a 6 GiB tmpfs at `/usr/share/games`, as 80-41 did.
3. The second boot, with the committed `boot.cfg`'s `intel_iommu=off`, the speed gate's configuration (decision E): `conformance.sh probe gate`, and `games.sh 0ad` with the clock pinned.
4. Debian left as found: the games purged with what they pulled in, the tmpfs unmounted, the session's files removed; p1 put back to `e21e697a...` after each boot, the menu emptied with `bootctl config`, `mixer vol 1`, `machine_identity.sh` again.

The WebGL chunks first ran the suite's 793 `deqp/` pages, which step 9's reference had skipped, at some 14 s a page: about three hours a browser. The chat decided at 17:34 to leave them out of Linux's reference and Rach2's cases alike. Chromium's chunk 4 finished as ordered; the remaining 19 chunks ran with `tests/graphics/runners/webgl-lenovo.omit`.
