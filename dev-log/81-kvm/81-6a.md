# 81-6a: Rach2's PCI devices found by one walk

The measurements of 81-6a.2 and 81-6a.3: Rach2's boot to its login prompt under KVM in WSL on the testing PC, the second level, before and after the walk and the SB16 change, with step 7's harness and image (`dev-log/76-graphics/research/run2/step5-7.md`, Boots to the login prompt).

## Setup

* Kernels, x86-64, built on the Mac: `base`, the milestone branch's head before this task (SHA-256 e72a9873...); `walk`, with the one walk alone (45ee5182...); `head`, with the walk and the SB16 change (87214d7f...); and `s7once`, step 7's kernel with its patch, `pci-once.diff` (33821213...), booted again beside them.
* Image: step 7's own `os-simple.krffs` (51934362...), from `/home/testing/rach2/build/cache/run2/step5-7/boot/once/`, so that only the kernel differs from step 7's boots.
* QEMU 10.2.1 of Ubuntu 26.04 in WSL, `-accel kvm -nodefaults -cpu max -smp 1 -m 128M`, the `pc` machine, booted to `login:` by `rach2boot.py` five times per kernel, which reads KVM's own counts through QMP's `query-stats`; then one boot per kernel traced by QEMU's `memory_region_ops_read` and `memory_region_ops_write` events (`tracecount.py`).
* The host was not idle: the load average read 4.5 to 5.3 before each kernel's boots, with two other accounts' QEMU guests and a `cppcheck` busy. Step 7's boots ran on an idle host. The traced boots ran at 23:31 UTC+6 on 2026-11-09, read from the guests' RTC lines; the timed boots just before them.

* 81-6a.3: the walk alone costs 107,955 to 126,720 exits a boot, below step 7's 148,401 to 149,035 with its patch, and boots in 0.939 s, within reach of step 7's 0.914 to 0.954 s on a loaded host. Step 7's own patched kernel, booted beside it, took 1.159 s and 119,235 to 138,771 exits, so the walk is no dearer than that patch under the same load. The walk reads 1,380 PCI registers a boot where step 7's patch read 17,278: the 32 slots of the one bus that exists and the 255 reads of device 0 of the others, in place of all 8,192 slots.
* 81-6a.2: the SB16 probe reads port 0x80 400 times a boot without the card, against 40,400 before: 100 reads of the reset's hold at each of the four bases, and none after the data port reads 0xFF. The probe's reads of the bases' own ports, 809 before, leave the list of the most read regions. With both changes a boot takes 0.658 s and 55,878 to 75,399 exits.
* The exits of one kernel spread by 5 to 35 percent between boots on this loaded host, against under 1 percent in step 7's on an idle one. The spread is in the port I/O exits, most of which KVM answers in its own kernel (`head`'s 50,270 to 69,421 against the 18,943 accesses that reach QEMU), and was not traced further.

## The devices found

* The boot logs of `base`, `walk` and `head` in the traced boots differ only in the RTC's second, the APIC timer's calibration count and `head`'s and `walk`'s new line `pci: list: 4 functions`.
* One boot of `base` and of `head` on each of `pc` and `q35`, with an e1000 behind a PCI-to-PCI bridge (`pci-bridge` on `pc`, `pcie-root-port` on `q35`), an Intel HDA on a second root bus, bus 64 (`pxb` on `pc`, `pxb-pcie` with a root port on `q35`), and a `qemu-xhci`: both kernels found the e1000, the HDA codec at 41:0.0, the xHCI and, on `q35`, the AHCI controller, with the same lines, but for the addresses of AHCI's command lists. `head` logged `pci: list: bus 64 is a root of its own` and `pci: list: 10 functions` on both machines.
* The Lenovo's boot with the walk is 81-6d's step.
