# 80-76: 0 A.D.'s EXECBUFFER2, mmap and newfstatat by call path

This task gives the profile four frames and the entry each sample was taken in, profiles 0 A.D. again on the Lenovo, and fixes what that shows.

## The profile

`bkl/profile` ([debugfs(5)](../../man/man5/debugfs.5), [the process subsystem](../../rach2/sys/proc/Readme.md)) keeps each sample's instruction and the three return addresses above it, and the entry the sampled process is in: `syscall N`, `vector N`, `ioctl` and its request, or `kernel 0` for none. The entry is kept in the process's record. A processor that holds the big lock keeps it across a switch, so the hold's own entry, which `bkl/calls` charges, names whichever call took the lock, which may be another process's. Each router therefore marks the entry in the process's record before its handlers and puts the outer one back after them (`bkl_entry_push` and `bkl_entry_pop`, in `isr_router_locked` and `irq_dispatch`); a call that sleeps keeps its mark and gets it back on any processor, and a forked child, whose record starts zeroed, reads `kernel 0` until its first entry. The table has 16384 slots, eight times 80-41's 2048, which lost 14% of 0 A.D.'s samples with two frames and no entry: 768 KiB of the x86-64 kernel's BSS and 448 KiB of i686's. A line is

```
ioctl 0x40406469 0xffffffff8024b1d0 0xffffffff80250c44 0xffffffff8024e9a8 0xffffffff80245f10 12
```

[`tests/system/games/bklread.sh`](../../tests/system/games/bklread.sh) names the samples on the host against the `kernel.debug` that took them: the samples of each entry, and for each entry asked for its 20 heaviest functions and 40 heaviest paths of four frames, with each one's share of the entry.

## The check in QEMU

The x86-64 kernel of 1576fa7a0 booted under KVM on the testing host with four processors and 2 GiB, with the os-real RAM image of the main clone, and ran as root `ls -lR /` and 300 `stty -a` in each of four shells at once, with `bkl/profile` and `bkl/calls` zeroed before and read after. The profile held 13,181 samples and lost none, in 331 lines, each with its entry and four frames: 12,998 samples under 329 lines of system calls and 183 under two ioctl requests, 0x5413 and 0x5401, the TIOCGWINSZ and TCGETS of `stty`. `bklread.sh` named them: lstat (syscall 6) 5,672 samples, 43%, of which 1,855 under `_krffs_memcpy <- krffs_memory_read <- krffs_brief <- krffs_find_child`, the RAM image's directory walk, and 3,442 at `arch_irq_enable <- _syscall_handler <- _dispatch <- isr_router_locked`, the waits for the lock at the call's entry, as 80-41 read them; writev (20) 4,295. Both kernels compile; the times read 0 in QEMU, which measured no TSC rate.

A sample at `arch_irq_enable` under `_syscall_handler`, or at `kirq_restore` under `schedule`, is a tick that came while the processor spun for the lock with interrupts off, so it stands for a wait of any length rather than for work; the reading below leaves those paths out of an entry's work. A sample at `kirq_restore` under `spin_unlock_irqrestore` is work done with interrupts off under that spinlock, and its three frames now name the caller and two above it.
