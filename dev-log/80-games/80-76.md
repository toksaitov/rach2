# 80-76: 0 A.D.'s EXECBUFFER2, mmap and newfstatat by call path

80-41 ([80-41.md](80-41.md)) left three excesses over Linux in 0 A.D. that its counters do not explain: some 700 us of each I915_GEM_EXECBUFFER2 call's 1,077 us held against Linux's 119 us of wall time, once the handle table and the command parser's lookup are fixed; mmap at 144 us against 16; and newfstatat at 22 us against 2.6. Its profile kept two frames, so it could not name the callers of `spin_unlock_irqrestore`, 24,319 of 0 A.D.'s samples, and it tied no sample to its system call. This task gives the profile four frames and the entry each sample was taken in, profiles 0 A.D. again on the Lenovo, and fixes what that shows.

## The profile

`bkl/profile` ([debugfs(5)](../../man/man5/debugfs.5), [the process subsystem](../../rach2/sys/proc/Readme.md)) keeps each sample's instruction and the three return addresses above it, and the entry the sampled process is in: `syscall N`, `vector N`, `ioctl` and its request, or `kernel 0` for none. The entry is kept in the process's record. A processor that holds the big lock keeps it across a switch, so the hold's own entry, which `bkl/calls` charges, names whichever call took the lock, which may be another process's. Each router therefore marks the entry in the process's record before its handlers and puts the outer one back after them (`bkl_entry_push` and `bkl_entry_pop`, in `isr_router_locked` and `irq_dispatch`); a call that sleeps keeps its mark and gets it back on any processor, and a forked child, whose record starts zeroed, reads `kernel 0` until its first entry. The table has 16384 slots, eight times 80-41's 2048, which lost 14% of 0 A.D.'s samples with two frames and no entry: 768 KiB of the x86-64 kernel's BSS and 448 KiB of i686's. A line is

```
ioctl 0x40406469 0xffffffff8024b1d0 0xffffffff80250c44 0xffffffff8024e9a8 0xffffffff80245f10 12
```

[`tests/system/games/bklread.sh`](../../tests/system/games/bklread.sh) names the samples on the host against the `kernel.debug` that took them: the samples of each entry, and for each entry asked for its 20 heaviest functions and 40 heaviest paths of four frames, with each one's share of the entry.

The profile held 13,181 samples and lost none, in 331 lines, each with its entry and four frames: 12,998 samples under 329 lines of system calls and 183 under two ioctl requests, 0x5413 and 0x5401, the TIOCGWINSZ and TCGETS of `stty`. `bklread.sh` named them: lstat (syscall 6) 5,672 samples, 43%, of which 1,855 under `_krffs_memcpy <- krffs_memory_read <- krffs_brief <- krffs_find_child`, the RAM image's directory walk, and 3,442 at `arch_irq_enable <- _syscall_handler <- _dispatch <- isr_router_locked`, the waits for the lock at the call's entry, as 80-41 read them; writev (20) 4,295. Both kernels compile; the times read 0 in QEMU, which measured no TSC rate.

A sample at `arch_irq_enable` under `_syscall_handler`, or at `kirq_restore` under `schedule`, is a tick that came while the processor spun for the lock with interrupts off, so it stands for a wait of any length rather than for work; the reading below leaves those paths out of an entry's work. A sample at `kirq_restore` under `spin_unlock_irqrestore` is work done with interrupts off under that spinlock, and its three frames now name the caller and two above it.

The fixes, compiled for both architectures and not yet measured on the Lenovo:

* 80-76.9 (#4054, 4b94f049e): `find_free_frame` and `alloc_kernel_frame` search through an index of the free frames, a count for each group of 64 and a bit for each group with one, and pass 32 empty groups in one word.

* 80-76.11 (#4056, 4a94d7af0): EXECBUFFER2 takes its shadow from the GT's pool, whose idle objects keep their pages and binding; the retire pass drops those a second idle, the park every idle one.
* 80-76.12 (#4057, c5b43d899): mmap of a file leaves its name to the first reading of `/proc/PID/maps`, `smaps` or `numa_maps`.
* 80-76.13 (#4058, 7ef59fa6b): a sleep lock's take reads the cycle counter, and the hold is converted only when it crossed a disk wait.
