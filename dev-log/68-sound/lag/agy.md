### 1. Ranked Causes and Arithmetic on Codec `0x14F1510F`

* `hda: codec capability word`: In [`hda_rates`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L1505-L1549), `pcm 0x000A0560` has rate bits `0x0560` (bits 5, 6, 8, 10: 44,100, 48,000, 96,000, and 192,000 Hz) and size bits `0x000A0000` (bits 17 and 19: 16-bit and 24-bit PCM); `formats 0x00000001` is standard PCM ([hda.c](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L1516)). The codec does not support 11,025 Hz or 8-bit PCM.
* `hda: programmed stream`: [`PCSoundInit`](file://ports/doom/i_pcsound.c#L214-L219) requests 11,025 Hz, `AUDIO_U8`, mono (11,025 B/s). On the write path, [`hda_start`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2412-L2427) forces 16-bit stereo (`AUDIO_S16LE`, 4 B/frame) and calls [`hda_select_hw_rate`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L1918-L1931), which picks the lowest supported rate when all exceed 11,025 Hz: **44,100 Hz** (format `0x4011`, 176,400 B/s), with `resample_step_fp = 0x4000` (0.25 input frames per output frame, [`hda_rate_step`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L1934-L1949)).
* `hda + doom: rank 1 (457.1 ms to 742.9 ms) -- unpaced 8 KiB input ring filled by wall-clock catch-up`: [`hda_ring`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L117-L120) holds `HDA_RING_SIZE - 1 = 8191` bytes of **unresampled input** ([`ring_free`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L326-L329)). At 11,025 B/s, 8,191 bytes is $8191 / 11025 = 742.9\text{ ms}$. Whenever Doom stalls without calling [`PCSoundUpdate`](file://ports/doom/i_pcsound.c#L295-L339), [`ring_render_to_hw_buffer`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L395-L398) outputs silence without advancing `ring_read_ptr`, while `last_audio_ms` falls behind wall time. During the ~1 s screen wipe in [`D_Display`](file://ports/doom/src/d_main.c#L308-L328), `S_UpdateSounds` is never called; on the first tic after the wipe, `PCSoundUpdate` sees `elapsed_ms >= 1000` and writes `MAX_CATCHUP_SAMPLES = 5040` bytes ($5040 / 11025 = 457.1\text{ ms}$, [i_pcsound.c](file://ports/doom/i_pcsound.c#L105-L331)) of stale music into `hda_ring`. A second stall or wipe saturates `hda_ring` at 8,191 bytes ($742.9\text{ ms}$). Because `PCSoundUpdate` then produces at 11,025 B/s and HDA consumes at $44100 \times 0.25 = 11025\text{ B/s}$, that backlog never drains. A new shot mixed in `PCSoundUpdate` is appended to the tail of `hda_ring` behind 457.1 to 742.9 ms of queued audio. (In QEMU, [`sb16_start`](file://rach2/sys/audio/sb16.c#L503-L504) caps `ring_size` at `1024` bytes = 92.9 ms, dropping the rest of the 5,040-byte burst.)
* `hda: rank 2 (92.9 ms to 185.8 ms, avg 139.3 ms) -- two 16 KiB DMA periods`: [`HDA_BUFFER_SIZE`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L111-L114) is 16,384 bytes (4,096 stereo S16LE frames) and `HDA_NUM_BUFFERS` is 2 (`cbl = 32768`). At 44,100 Hz, one period is $4096 / 44100 = 92.88\text{ ms}$. [`hda_service_stream`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L523-L547) refills only on period boundaries (`hw_period != hw_period_last`), rendering 4,096 frames (1,024 input bytes) into `next_buf`. Once the shot's bytes reach `ring_read_ptr`, they wait 0 to 92.88 ms for `current_buf` to finish so they are rendered into `next_buf`, then wait a full 92.88 ms while the other buffer plays before LPIB reaches `next_buf`.
* `doom: rank 3 (0 to 28.6 ms, avg 14.3 ms) -- 35 Hz tic quantization`: Total delay on the Lenovo is $457.1 + 139.3 + 14.3 = 610.7\text{ ms}$ after one wipe, and $742.9 + 139.3 + 14.3 = 896.5\text{ ms}$ (up to $957.3\text{ ms}$) once `hda_ring` is full.

### 2. Rest of the Path from Key Press to DAC

* `input: keyboard path (0 to 10 ms)`: Laptop PS/2 key presses fire an IRQ immediately ([`input.c`](file://rach2/sys/input.c#L361-L384)); USB keyboards are polled on the 100 Hz tick ([`kernel.c`](file://rach2/kernel.c#L304), 0 to 10 ms). [`kd_raw_code`](file://rach2/sys/kd.c#L595-L600) queues the scancode to `console_tty`, which [`poll_console_keys`](file://ports/doom/doomgeneric_rach2.c#L278-L293) reads non-blocking every frame and inside [`DG_GetKey`](file://ports/doom/doomgeneric_rach2.c#L541-L548). (Note: [`I_GetEvent`](file://ports/doom/src/i_input.c#L322) breaks out of its `while` loop after the first key release, delaying any key press queued behind a release by one tic.)
* `doom: engine sound update (0 to 28.6 ms)`: [`doomgeneric_Tick`](file://ports/doom/src/d_main.c#L405-L419) runs `TryRunTics`, `S_UpdateSounds`, and `D_Display` in sequence. [`TryRunTics`](file://ports/doom/src/d_loop.c#L767-L785) sleeps in 1 ms steps until the next 1/35 s (28.57 ms) tic, reads the key, runs [`A_WeaponReady`](file://ports/doom/src/p_pspr.c#L306-L315) -> [`S_StartSound`](file://ports/doom/src/s_sound.c#L391-L407) -> [`PCSoundStartSound`](file://ports/doom/i_pcsound.c#L492-L540) (which only marks the channel active), and then `S_UpdateSounds` mixes and writes the first chunk before `D_Display` draws the muzzle flash in the same tick.
* `doom: module clock and catch-up`: [`DG_GetTicksMs`](file://ports/doom/doomgeneric_rach2.c#L524-L533) uses `gettimeofday` ([`syscall.c`](file://rach2/sys/syscall.c#L1038-L1041)), which is exact, but `PCSoundUpdate` generates `elapsed_ms * 11025 / 1000` bytes by wall-clock delta without checking how much audio is already queued in the kernel.
* `hda: service cadence and DMA buffer (0 to 10 ms)`: [`hda_service_stream`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L484-L547) runs from both [`hda_irq_handler`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L1558-L1583) (MSI on BDL `ioc = 1`, [hda.c](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2456)) and [`hda_tick`](file://rach2/kernel.c#L300) at 100 Hz.

### 3. Fix, Target Latency, Safety, and Queue Interface

* `hda: shrink DMA period and bound ring by duration`:
  * Lower [`HDA_BUFFER_SIZE`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L111) from `16384` to `4096` bytes (1,024 stereo S16LE frames = **23.22 ms** per period at 44.1 kHz, **21.33 ms** at 48 kHz; 128-byte aligned for HDA BDL), or `2048` bytes (**11.61 ms** at 44.1 kHz) once the 1000 Hz tick is in place.
  * In [`hda_start`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2412), set an active `ring_size` proportional to the input byte rate (as [`sb16_start`](file://rach2/sys/audio/sb16.c#L503-L504) does): 4 DMA periods of input bytes (at 11,025 B/s and 23.2 ms periods, `1024` bytes = **92.9 ms** instead of 8,191 bytes = 742.9 ms).
* `audio + syscall: queue-delay interface`:
  * Add `int (*get_delay)(void)` to [`audio_driver_t`](file://rach2/sys/audio/audio.h#L39-L84) and ioctl `AUDIO_GET_DELAY` (`0x4106`, analogous to OSS `SNDCTL_DSP_GETODELAY` and ALSA `snd_pcm_delay`) in [`syscall.c`](file://rach2/sys/syscall.c#L597-L639), returning total unplayed input bytes across the software ring and DMA buffer: on HDA, `ring_available()` plus the unplayed frames in both DMA periods (`2 * HDA_BUFFER_SIZE - (lpib % HDA_BUFFER_SIZE)`) scaled from `hw_rate` to `current_sample_rate`.
  * A music player uses `AUDIO_GET_DELAY` to pace writes and compute playback position (`written - queued`).
* `doom: cap catch-up and top up to target lead`:
  * In [`PCSoundUpdate`](file://ports/doom/i_pcsound.c#L105-L338), lower `MAX_CATCHUP_SAMPLES` from `315 * 16` (457 ms) to `315 * 2 = 630` bytes (**57.1 ms**, 2 tics), and use `AUDIO_GET_DELAY` so `PCSoundUpdate` generates at most what is needed to keep ~2 tics (630 input bytes) queued in the ring. Also remove the `break` after `ev_keyup` in [`I_GetEvent`](file://ports/doom/src/i_input.c#L322).
* `hda + doom: target latency and underrun safety`:
  * With `HDA_BUFFER_SIZE = 4096` (23.2 ms period, 34.8 ms average 1.5-period DMA delay) and a 1-to-2 tic ring lead (0 to 28.6 ms standing in `hda_ring` before a new tic writes), shot-to-DAC latency drops from ~900 ms to **~50 to 78 ms** (and **~35 to 60 ms** with `HDA_BUFFER_SIZE = 2048` under the 1000 Hz tick).
  * This is safe against underruns on the Lenovo: MSI services `next_buf` immediately at each 23.2 ms period boundary, and even if an MSI is missed, the 100 Hz `hda_tick` polls every 10 ms (at least two ticks inside every 23.2 ms period). On the userland side, 2 tics (57.1 ms) of buffered audio covers Doom's 28.6 ms tic cadence with a full tic of margin.

### 4. Measuring the Delay Before and After by Counters

* `procfs + hda: expose queue and DMA counters in /proc/audio`:
  * [`hda_debug_dump_stats`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2241-L2301) already tracks `ring_available()`, `ring_high_water`, `session_bytes_written`, `session_bytes_dropped`, `underrun_count`, `cbl`, and `lpib`. Print those fields in [`hda_proc_status`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2303-L2338) so `/proc/audio` can be sampled while Doom runs.
  * Instantaneous audio path delay in ms from `/proc/audio` counters is:
    $$\text{delay\_ms} = \frac{\text{ring\_avail} \times 1000}{\text{requested\_rate}} + \frac{(2 \times \text{HDA\_BUFFER\_SIZE} - (\text{lpib} \bmod \text{HDA\_BUFFER\_SIZE})) \times 1000}{\text{programmed\_rate} \times 4}$$
  * Before the fix, after the startup screen wipe, `ring_avail` reads `5040` to `8191` (`457` to `743 ms`) and the DMA term averages `139 ms`. After the fix, `ring_avail` stays $\le 630$ (`0` to `57 ms`) and the DMA term averages `35 ms`.
* `kd + hda: end-to-end key-to-DAC counter in HDA WALCLK ticks`:
  * Read the 24 MHz hardware wall clock `hda_read32(HDA_WALCLK)` (24,000 ticks/ms, [`hda.c`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2083)) at three points: `t_key` in [`kd_raw_code`](file://rach2/sys/kd.c#L595) on Ctrl press (`code == 0x1D`), `t_write` in [`hda_write`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L2749) when the first post-`t_key` buffer is queued at `w_shot = ring_write_ptr`, and `t_render` in [`hda_service_stream`](file://rach2/sys/audio/hda.c#L539) when `ring_read_ptr` passes `w_shot` at frame `f_shot` of `next_buf`, adding the remaining DMA frames $((HDA\_BUFFER\_SIZE - (\text{lpib} \bmod HDA\_BUFFER\_SIZE))/4 + f_{\text{shot}}) / 44.1\text{ ms}$.
