# 87-6: The fair scheduler measured against Linux

The figures of the plan's Done that 87-6 measures, each read from its run's own output: the wake figure in QEMU (87-6a), Firefox's thread waits in QEMU (87-6b, run as task 87-10), and the Lenovo's gate and WineD3D's round trip on a trial boot (87-6c). Times are UTC+6.

## 87-6a: Wakes

The workload is the `wakes` row of `schedsmpmusl`, the program of 87-7's `tests/run sched4`, at `task/5487`'s 3a4e05140: a process pinned to processor 1 sleeps 1 ms and runs 0.1 ms, timed by the monotonic clock, 2000 times, beside a nice-0 spinner pinned to each of the four processors, with `kernel.sched_schedstats` at 1. The figure is the sleeper's `wait_sum` over `wait_count` from its `/proc/PID/sched`. Both kernels ran it on the testing host under KVM, with QEMU 10.2.1, the processor `host,invtsc=on`, 4 processors and 512 MiB, on 2026-11-19 at about 19:20, three runs each.

* Linux: Debian 13's 6.12.115+deb13.1-amd64 in the reference guest, `debian-ref.sh run -c 4 -m 512`, the program built there with gcc -O2 and run as root in the root cgroup, with autogroup on. The host's load average was 9.8 at the start, with 8 other claims in the admission ledger.
* Rach2: the head of `milestone/76-display`, 9eae346d4, with `task/5487` merged for the program, the x86-64 kernel booted by `tests/run sched4` with `RACH2_QEMU_ACCEL=kvm` and the case's arguments cut to `-w 126777 wakes`. The merge also carried 87-7's two kernel commits, which change a suspend to idle and no wake path.

| Run | Linux waits | Linux mean | Rach2 waits | Rach2 mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2011 | 134,709 ns | 2014 | 129,466 ns |
| 2 | 2006 | 131,508 ns | 2007 | 136,168 ns |
| 3 | 2006 | 167,537 ns | 2008 | 130,250 ns |

The Done allows Rach2 25% or 0.5 ms above Linux, whichever is larger. Linux's median, 134,709 ns, gives a bound of 634,709 ns; Rach2's largest mean, 136,168 ns, is within 1.1% of Linux's median, and its median, 130,250 ns, is below it. The figure holds.

Before 87-4's activation merged, loop s measured 1.70 ms on a scratch merge of 87-4's and 87-5's branches under the same row (#5487), and Linux 126,612 to 127,439 ns with the program before 3a4e05140, which timed the sleeper's 0.1 ms by the thread's CPU clock.
