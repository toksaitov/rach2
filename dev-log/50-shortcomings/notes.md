The device model found seven missing barriers and one wrong undo in the FAT drivers. The undo of a failed join freed the run while the tail still named it in both drivers; FAT32 wrote a directory's first cluster after the entry that published it, grew a directory before the run's zeros were on the medium, renamed a name over the old one's slots before they were freed on the medium, freed a removed directory's chain before its entry was gone, cut a shrunk file's tail under a size that still covered it, and grew a file across a cluster with the join in the cache under a size that counted on it; exFAT retired a rename's old names after the new one was published, answered fsync from the cache, and had the same shrink and grow faults. ### The send path, measured on the machine

UDP tells us where the core goes. The same sender over UDP reaches 73 MB/s at 1400 bytes a datagram, also at 100 percent, so TCP itself adds about a fifth and the rest is the path both protocols share. Sweeping the datagram size, 33 MB/s at 256 bytes, 56 at 512 and 77 at 1400, fits a fixed cost of about 5.3 us a frame plus about 9 ns a byte, which is 110 MB/s of per-byte work. A gigabit at full frames needs a frame every 12.3 us and this path spends 18.
