# Shared build and tooling caches

The testing host shares stamped build outputs through `/var/cache/rach2-store` and prepared tooling bases through `/var/cache/rach2-tooling`. Explicit private-cache overrides remain valid. The release runner keeps its own GNU build, as the owner decided on [the cache issue](https://github.com/toksaitov/rach2/issues/893#issuecomment-6357868372).

The [build-store acceptance](https://github.com/toksaitov/rach2/issues/893#issuecomment-6362619116) records a new copy taking all six vendored projects from the store on both architectures, two copies taking each GNU step from the store, and the GNU checks passing on images made from those layers. The [root audit](https://github.com/toksaitov/rach2/issues/893#issuecomment-6372111901) removed the last two idle private GNU layers after archiving their logs, stamps and downloads. The remaining worker and owner layers were links into the store, 20 to 164 KiB per layer. The two evidence archives recovered from those layers have SHA-256 `87f542c7507068c56d2baf48814de8bb5aab8debfd0949611283f84dba9b6c74` and `bcdc645a7b3b423e0a4e8a999ea391316ad222fdf533079b5d4ba75d360a0273`.

The tooling directory is root:users, mode 2770. Normal preparation selects it only after checking its type, owner, mode and the caller's access. One kernel mutex per recipe serializes preparation and its reuse check. Mutexes have mode 0660 under a restrictive umask; published images, bases and reports have mode 0444. Private job references retain their bases, and pruning skips a busy preparation. Interrupted reports and pinned downloads remain available.

The focused native acceptance used the integrated cache source `5576f26908fbe2fbacd96b0042ee9a8605bac3670f196a81d85b897079d79e27` and committed fixture `bf056235ba279c4601b19c38950314925e3bc4ae8ccded54f785db94fa9442a2`. Under installed build admission, separate children ran as testing (uid 1000) and toksaitov-d (uid 1003). They passed the permission, cross-account access, private-reference, busy-prune, waiting-cancellation, killed-owner and subsequent obsolete-output checks. The command exited 0, removed all fixture scratch and left the admission ledger empty. No QEMU or suite ran. The issue retains the result excerpt; `build/tests/portable-batch/shared-tooling-cache/` retains the driver, archive, commands, logs and hashed receipts.
