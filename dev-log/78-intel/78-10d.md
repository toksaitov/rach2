# 78-10d: the display on the Lenovo

The first trial of the Intel driver's display on the Lenovo's HD 4400, as root: the adoption of the firmware's picture at boot, the eDP panel at thirteen modes through the fitter with the pipe's CRC of one pattern read at each, the backlight's levels, IGT's KMS subtests from `tests/graphics/igt-kms-trial.txt` with kms_hdmi_inject's 4K mode on DDI B, and the hotplug pins quiet with nothing plugged in. Linux's reference comes from a Debian boot of its own on p5 that runs the same CRC tool, the same backlight levels and the same IGT list; the Rach2 part is a trial root of the GNU system booted once from the data partition with `rach2.i915`, the driver with its display. Both boots take the screen.

## Review note (78-10d.1)

The display paths the trial runs for the first time on the chip, read against Linux 6.12.115's `drivers/gpu/drm/i915/display/` with Haswell's branches taken: the readout and adoption (`intel_modeset_setup.c`, `intel_plane_initial.c`), the mode set's two orders (`hsw_crtc_enable`, `hsw_crtc_disable`, `intel_ddi.c`'s pre-enable, enable, disable and post-disable), the transcoder's timings, M/N and DDI function, the fitter (`pch_panel_fitting`, `ilk_pfit_enable`), the colour (`ivb_csc_mode`, `ilk_load_lut`), the planes and watermarks, the DPLLs and port clocks (`intel_dpll_mgr.c`), the AUX channel (`intel_dp_aux.c`), the panel power sequencer (`intel_pps.c`), link training, the backlight (`intel_backlight.c`), HDMI and its infoframes, hotplug (`intel_hotplug.c`, `intel_hotplug_irq.c`), the pipe CRC (`intel_pipe_crc.c`) and the FIFO underruns (`intel_fifo_underrun.c`). Nine faults were fixed on `task/2658`; the rest match, apart from the differences listed after them.

### Fixed

* The second M/N, `PIPE_DATA_M2`, `N2`, `LINK_M2` and `N2`, was written on every DisplayPort transcoder. Haswell has it on the eDP transcoder alone (`intel_cpu_transcoder_has_m2_n2`); on transcoder A to C those offsets are other registers. It is now written for the eDP transcoder only (34c296e4a).
* `PIPE_CSC_MODE` was `CSC_POSITION_BEFORE_GAMMA` with the YUV bits for every output. `ivb_csc_mode` gives full range the matrix before the gamma and limited range, an HDMI mode's default, the matrix after it, with Linux's limited range matrix. Both now match (34c296e4a).
* `hsw_mode_set_planes_workaround` was missing: when one pipe comes on while another is already on, Linux waits two vblanks of the other before the planes. It now does (34c296e4a).
* A DisplayPort port's disable stopped DDI A's buffer before the transcoder's clock; `intel_ddi_post_disable_dp` stops the clock first on Haswell. The order now matches (34c296e4a).
* The backlight's enable took a level below the VBT's least as the least; `intel_backlight_enable` takes a level at or below it as the maximum (4d4e20b27).
* `intel_backlight`'s levels were the PWM's own. Linux's `scale_user_to_hw` maps 0 to the period onto the VBT's least to the period, rounded to the closest, `scale_hw_to_user` reads them back, level 0 turns the backlight off through its power switch, and `actual_brightness` reads 0 while it is off. Rach2 now does the same, and the backlight core reads the level again when the driver takes the device over at the bind (0ed3c00a7).
* `crc/data` gave one value a frame; i915 gives five, the CRC and four zeros (`values_cnt` 5), which IGT's `igt_pipe_crc` compares. It now gives five (fb704128d).
* An AUX transfer on an external DisplayPort port with nothing on its hotplug line ran the channel to its timeout. `intel_dp_aux_xfer` refuses it with ENXIO, which IGT's connector probes on DDI B and C reach. It now does (8ad53e8e0).
* FIFO underruns were not reported. Linux's `intel_fifo_underrun.c` starts an adopted pipe unreported, reports from the start of each mode set or the first commit of the firmware's picture, logs `CPU pipe A FIFO underrun` once and masks the error interrupt. Rach2 now does the same, so the trial's log shows an underrun as Linux's would (61eeb001d).
* The commits' documents: the i915 Readme, `rach2/sys/display/Readme.md`, `man/man4/i915.4` and `man/man4/backlight.4` (c355a1f48 and the commits above).

### Differences left

* The pipe CRC is read by the vblank's deferred work from `PIPE_CRC_RES`, where Linux reads it on `GEN7_ERR_INT`'s CRC done bit. Both read the same register once a frame, and the frame number in `crc/data` is the vblank counter in both, so the values and their order are the same; the error interrupt is kept for the underruns.
* The panel power sequencer's power cycle field is read and written as Linux 6.18 does, the delay in 100 ms plus one, which is how the hardware counts it; 6.12 reads the field whole and adds 100 ms, and so waits 600 ms for the Lenovo's 500. Its waits for the sequencer read it every 10 ms for at most 5 s, as 6.18's `wait_panel_status`, where 6.12 backs off from 10 us to 1 ms.
* HDMI audio is not driven, so kms_hdmi_inject's `inject-audio` is left out of the list.

### Readiness

* What runs that 78-4b and 78-5c did not: 78-5c booted with `rach2.i915.disable_display`, so this is the first boot that runs `intel_display_init` on the chip. At the bind it reads the pipes, transcoders, DDI, PLLs, fitter and planes, and writes only the panel power sequencer's delays, the AUX channel's messages to the panel, the low-power watermarks off and the hotplug pins of DDI B and C; pipe A, its link and the backlight are written only when a program commits. The mode sets, the CRC, the backlight's levels and HDMI on DDI B run only from the step's programs, after the log has been read.
* Every register sequence of those paths was checked against 6.12 line by line, as above.
* Every wait is bounded and logs when it reaches its bound: a vblank at 100 ms (`no vblank in 100 ms`), the panel power sequencer's states at 5 s (`pps: ...: timeout after`), an AUX message at 10 ms with five sends and the DPCD's 32 tries, a DDI buffer's idle state (`ddi: timeout waiting for DDI BUF`), the DisplayPort idle patterns and a pipe's off state (`timed out`). The host test's rows find none of these lines. No wait runs in the interrupt handler.
* Interrupts: `i915_irq_register` leaves the display engine's vblank, flip done and error bits enabled but masked; vblank is unmasked only while a CRTC's vblank is on, flip done while a flip waits for it, the error interrupt only once a commit has turned the underruns of every pipe on, and the PCH's hotplug bits once the hotplug work is ready. The handler takes the events under the mask's lock and leaves the rest to the deferred work.
* What the chip can reach: the planes scan out of framebuffers pinned in the global GTT, the cursor out of a pinned object; the CRC and status registers are read only. A wrong plane address reads the scratch page, which shows black.
* What a fault costs: a mode set that leaves the panel dark or a frozen picture does not stop the processor; SSH still answers, and the put-back reboot gives the firmware's picture back. kms_hdmi_inject drives DDI B with no monitor on it, as it does under Linux.
* Host test, run alone at 61eeb001d and again after the merge of the video rings, 1c6ade1de: `tests/host/i915_kms_trial_hosttest.c`, the trial's path on the chip model with DDI B and C strapped and no monitor, from the adoption without a write to pipe A, its link or the backlight, through the thirteen mode sets at Linux's fitter windows with three CRCs each, the underruns, the backlight's four levels, kms_hdmi_inject's 4K mode on pipe A through DDI B and ten quiet seconds, each row finding no wait at its bound in the log: `i915-kms-trial-test: ok`. Two mutants fail it as they should: the old colour mode and raw backlight levels fail the backlight and HDMI rows, and underruns left reported on the adopted pipe fail the adoption and underrun rows.
* Not tried in QEMU: QEMU has no Intel display, so a boot there cannot run these paths; the host test runs them on the chip model.

### Findings, each fixed on task/2658

* `crcmodes` kept the NUL that ends each line of `crc/data` in its buffer, and Linux refused every read once the buffer filled with them. It drops it.
* GET_PIPE_FROM_CRTC_ID was missing, and each CRTC's `i915_pipe` and `i915_current_bpc` in debugfs, without which IGT's `igt_display_require` stops every KMS test. All three are Linux's.
* A kernel thread that held pid 1 when init started, the DRM worker the display driver starts, left init another pid, and BusyBox's `reboot` signals pid 1. Such a thread now takes the next free pid.
* The firmware's sprite watermark, left in `WM0_PIPE_A`, made Weston's first commit fail. The watermarks of the adopted state are computed at the bind, as Linux's `ilk_wm_sanitize`.
* wpa_supplicant crashed every 5 seconds: Debian's libnl in the GNU root's `/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu`, which `ld.so.conf` searches first, shadowed the ports' libnl. The GNU root keeps Debian's libnl out.
* The pipe CRC was read at the vblank, where `PIPE_CRC_RES` still held the frame before, and the first line after a source started on a running pipe was a partial frame's. It is read at CRC done in the error interrupt, as `hsw_pipe_crc_irq_handler`, with the first frame dropped. This replaces the first of the review note's differences.
* ADDFB2 ignored the object's tiling, and SET_TILING changed an object a framebuffer was made of. ADDFB2 now checks the tiling, modifier, stride and size as `intel_framebuffer_init` does, and SET_TILING gives EBUSY meanwhile.
* A legacy page flip of a CRTC that DPMS had turned off returned 0; Linux's `page_flip_common` gives EINVAL.
* The cursor took only squares shown whole, and IGT's probe of a 64-pixel cursor on a 64x65 framebuffer was refused. It takes 8 lines to its width high from a framebuffer that may be larger, with its height in `CUR_FBC_CTL`, as `i9xx_cursor_size_ok` lets Haswell.
* Haswell's planes had `FB_DAMAGE_CLIPS`, which Linux gives only to display version 12 and later, so `atomic-plane-damage` ran here and skipped there. They have none; DIRTYFB still flushes.
* A cursor at INT_MIN came out visible, and `CURPOS`'s 15-bit magnitude put it at the left edge, failing `cursor-offscreen`. `drm_rect_clip_scaled` and `drm_rect_visible` wrap their differences to 32 bits as Linux's kernel does.
* Vblanks were stamped with the clock in the deferred work, whose delay spread the stamps by a standard deviation of 38 to 168 us, past `kms_flip`'s limit of three standard deviations within 0.5% of the frame. They are stamped with the end of the vertical blank from `PIPEDSL`, as `i915_get_crtc_scanoutpos`.
* MAP_DUMB of the firmware's framebuffer, above.
