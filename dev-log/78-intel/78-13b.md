# 78-13b: conformance against Linux

The Lenovo's GPU conformance cases, `tests/native/gpu.cases`: each runner of `tests/graphics` on the HD 4400 and the panel through `tests/graphics/runners/lenovo.sh`, judged by case against Linux's run on the same machine, and the speed gate against Linux with the IOMMU's translation off (decision E). The whole comparison runs at the release's Lenovo step, which runs every native case; this task builds it, and proves each runner on a small part in QEMU and then in one trial boot of Rach2 on the Lenovo. The references the second discovery run lacks came from 83-15's two Debian boots of 2026-11-12 ([83-15](../83-testbed/83-15.md)). `tests/graphics/Readme.md`'s Lenovo section describes the cases and their references.

## What was built

* 78-13b.1: `tests/native/gpu.cases`, 52 cases: dEQP-GLES2, GLES3 and GLES31 in 1, 2 and 3 parts; piglit's quick profile in 5 parts through GBM; IGT's buffer and submission list in 2 parts and its display list in 9; glmark2's validations, GL and GLES; the WebGL 2.0.1 suite in 12 chunks in each browser; the gate's glmark2 benchmarks and WebGL probes. `etude prepare native` and `etude run native` read the file beside the other three, and a program under `graphics/` is a script of `tests/`. Each case keeps under the collection's 30-minute bound at twice Linux's time: IGT's display parts hold at most 10 minutes of Linux's run, and `kms_flip`, 33 minutes alone, is split by subtest.
* The runners on the machine's own display: `RUNNER_SESSION=1` draws on the session's compositor (Rach2's desktop Weston on DRM with the GL renderer, as Linux's references were taken under Weston on the panel), glmark2 runs `--fullscreen` and takes `-a gl|es2` and `-m validate|benchmark`, piglit takes `-p gbm` with Linux's platform, and `probe.sh` measures the WebGL fill and ALU checks.
* 78-13b.2: `tests/graphics/lenovo-expect.py` makes `ref/lenovo/expect/igt-kms.rows` (2,188 results) and `piglit-quick.rows.gz` (63,601 results, the reruns' 9 setup passes in place) and `igt-lenovo-kms.list` from step 9's files; dEQP's cases judge against step 9's `results.csv.gz` as they stand. `judge.py results` takes a part's scope, and every judge but the gate takes the owner's exclusions.
* 78-13b.3: `ref/lenovo/gate`: a loss of at most 10%, glmark2 1387 GL and 1379 GLES from step 9b, and the probe's eight Linux values from 83-15's Debian boot with translation off, in which glmark2 scored 1387 and 1396 again.
* 78-13b.4: `tools/lenovo/debian-ref/conformance.sh`: in the Debian boot, with translation off, IGT's buffer list as root with no compositor, then Weston as gfx on the panel and on it glmark2's validations and benchmark, the probe and the WebGL suite whole, each through `lenovo.sh` with `-`; `collect` gathers `expect/igt-gem.rows`, the glmark2 validations, the two browsers' pages and the gate's lines. 83-15 ran it on the idle Lenovo and committed what it gathered.

This task adds no kernel code: its trial boots the milestone's head, whose driver paths the earlier Lenovo steps of 78 ran, and this step runs after all of them, as the plan orders. What it runs that no earlier step ran in this form, and why none of it can stop the machine:

* Every wait in the runners is a runner's own bounded readiness wait (600 tries of 0.1 s for a socket or a compositor's end) or the browser runners' 120 s of silence, as their Readme says; a stuck test leaves its process, never the processor, and the put-back's reboot ends it.
