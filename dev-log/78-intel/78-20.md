# 78-20: the kernel's log as UDP datagrams to the Mac

`netconsole=` sends each record of the kernel's log as one UDP datagram to each target, as Linux's netconsole and netpoll do (`rach2/sys/network/netconsole.c`, [netconsole(4)](../../man/man4/netconsole.4)), so that the Mac reads a Lenovo trial's log with `nc -lu` as the owner's user, with no root. This records the step on the Lenovo; the QEMU case is `machine.netconsole.udp`.

## The step

On 11 November, by the owner's loop on the Mac, under the manager's go: the x86-64 kernel of e0e98fbd8 with this task's commits, staged in the candidate slot with `bootctl kernel` and booted once from a menu block that [`78-20/candidate.sh`](78-20/candidate.sh) staged with the release's line and a `netconsole=` word. `nc -lu 6666` ran on the Mac as the owner's user, writing under `build/runs/t3582/`. The release, bb151e3ba with kernel `0effe5ac...`, was in both slots before and was put back in both after; `machine_identity.sh` found it in both each time.

## Results

Three boots, each silent and each answering SSH within 25 s.

1. 16:49, kernel `62ae45e9...`, `netconsole=6665@192.168.0.106/,6666@192.168.0.100/`: 3 of the boot's 229 records reached the Mac ([run 1](78-20/lenovo-netconsole-run1.log)). Two faults. The 8 KiB ring was full before the link came up at 8.235 s (`6853 characters dropped`), and it filled in the middle of a record, so the cut record `vtd: dmar: host address wid` was sent joined to the record of 9.359 s. And of the datagrams sent to the broadcast station address, Linux's default, most of the records after the link came up were missing too, the records of 10.279 s and 11.152 s among them. `2644eda4a` made the ring 32 KiB and has a full ring drop whole records.
2. 16:55, kernel `8c2c34dd...`, with the Mac's station address, `d0:11:e5:c8:0d:59` from the Lenovo's `/proc/net/arp`, as `tgt-macaddr`: no character dropped, and every record from 9.060 s on reached the Mac ([run 2](78-20/lenovo-netconsole-run2.log)). The boot log before it, some 220 records sent as one drain the moment the link came up at 8.227 s, did not, although the card counted no transmit error (`/proc/net/dev`: errs, fifo, colls and carrier 0). `71c9e82d4` holds a drain until its link has been up for two seconds, unless the kernel panics.
3. 17:02, kernel `5f7764fb...`, the same line: passed. The Mac's log ([whole](78-20/lenovo-netconsole.log)) holds the previous boot's shutdown records, then this boot's 242 records from `<6>[0.000000] boot: Rach2 Booting` on, among them `netconsole: udp: 6665@192.168.0.106/eth0,6666@192.168.0.100/D0:11:E5:C8:0D:59`, `netconsole: started: network logging, targets 1` and `netconsole: udp: port 6666 of eth0: 0 datagrams, held, 0 characters dropped`. The machine's own `dmesg` ([here](78-20/lenovo-dmesg.txt)) had kept the last 229; those are the Mac's last 229, in the same order, line for line once the level and the stamp's padding are set aside.

The machine skill's trial line now gives the Mac's station address, and names `en1`, the Mac's Wi-Fi port, for its IPv4 address.
