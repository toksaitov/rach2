# disk(7)

NAME: disk - the disks, their partitions, their nodes and their identities. Source: rach2/sys/block/Readme.md (Block devices and partitions, The partition service, The loop device, Partition discovery and the disk root, Raw disk devices, Disk policy and recovery authority, Disk information); Readme.md "What a fresh disk holds".

* Sectors are 512 bytes. A disk is found by the AHCI or ATA probe (AHCI first) or is a USB stick; the registry has eight records, numbered in registration order and reused from the lowest released. `/dev/diskN` is the whole disk (mode 0400 root's; published for every disk on a recovery kernel or with `rach2.diskdiag`, and for a stick always), `/dev/diskNpM` a partition (0400): M is 1 to 4 for MBR primary entries, 5 upward for logical partitions in chain order (the extended entry itself has no node), the array position from 1 for GPT. `/dev/bootpart` (0600) is MBR entry 1 when it is a FAT32 partition starting at or after LBA 2048, under 2 GiB, overlapping nothing; `/dev/grubcore` (0600) is LBA 1 to 2047 when no partition overlaps them. `stat` reports the disk and partition nodes as block devices, the two windows as character devices.
* Tables: MBR with one extended chain, or GPT (primary header, then the backup); entries out of range or overlapping are refused and counted; at most 15 partitions a disk.
* Identity: `rach2.root=SPEC` and `rach2.boot=SPEC` select by `PARTUUID=GUID`, `MBRID=XXXXXXXX,M`, `UUID=` (ext2 volume), `LABEL=` (GPT name or ext2 label), or `/dev/diskNpM`; exactly one partition must match, else no root is mounted and the kernel logs why. growroot(8) names its partition the same way.
* Writes: one write-capable descriptor per disk across its nodes (EBUSY for a second); a mount claims its range (a write-capable open over it answers EBUSY, and a mount over an open writer likewise). The ordinary kernel refuses every raw write with EPERM; the recovery kernel writes for root; the metal kernel's driver policy allows only reads. `BLKRRPART` on a whole-disk node rereads the table (EBUSY while a partition is mounted or another descriptor is open on the disk). A raw write to a write-protected stick: EIO.
* Reads through the nodes take any byte range; raw writes and boot-window reads need sector alignment; at most eight transfers with their own buffers at once.
* `/proc/disk`: the probe state, the driver policy, the counters (commands, flushes, polls, wait ticks, in flight, overlaps, quarantines, releases), disk 0's record with `part1` to `part4` lines, then one line per disk `disk N TRANSPORT LOCATION SECTORS TABLE MODEL` and `identity N ...` with model, serial and firmware; `part N ...` lines per partition.
* The sealed disk: partition 1 the FAT32 boot partition (GRUB, the kernel slots, the menu and the environment block), partition 2 the recovery system's image, partition 3 the ext2 root; the GRUB core image in the gap before partition 1.
* Loop devices: `/dev/loop0` to `/dev/loop3` over a regular file; see loopctl(8).
* SEE ALSO: rach2-install(8), bootctl(8), growroot(8), reflash(8), loopctl(8), mount(8), recovery(7).
