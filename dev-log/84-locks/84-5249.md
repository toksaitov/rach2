## Design

`intel_vblank.c`'s `err_int` masks `DE_ERR_INT_IVB` when it reports a FIFO underrun. That interrupt also carries pipe CRC completion. `intel_fifo_underrun_reporting` unmasks it only when every pipe's reporting is enabled again. An underrun left on C therefore stops CRCs on A and B until C's mode set restores its reporting. The reading meets the owner's condition for landing the prepared fix; it does not measure that fix's effect.

The prepared changes add Linux's `i915_fifo_underrun_reset` debugfs write for IGT and begin a fastset's PIPESRC, fitter and plane writes just after a vblank, so that the registers latch together. A commit that crosses a vblank logs the number crossed. Both kernels link. The XR24 parent and pipes A and B require Linux's passes again, in the stored expectations and their generator.
