# 84-266q: why machine.d3dvenus.vkd3d-7 failed in the release's second rerun

The release's second rerun failed `machine.d3dvenus.vkd3d-7` on x86_64 with "row failed: Direct3D row did not match Linux". Every unit that `run.py` judged matched; the row then died in the runner with `KeyError: 'failures'` at `counts[1] in expected["failures"]`.

## The unit

A vkd3d-proton unit reads `expected["failures"]` unless the reference's `placement` names it or its entry has `varying_lines`. Of the 578 units in `venus.json`, one has neither and no `failures`: `test_multithread_command_queue_exec`, whose entry is only `progress`, the record at which the Debian guest's runs on Venus stalled (its line 4176, a failed readback, counted 26 times). It is in row 7, the seventh of every seven tests after `test_create_device`. `counts` is not None only when the unit printed its summary, so on Rach2 the unit ran past Linux's stall to its end.

## The fix

The runner computed the failure check before it chose between the stalled unit's rule and the counted one's, so a stalled unit that ran to its summary reached the missing key. The Readme's rule is that such a unit matches when it gets to Linux's record or runs to its summary ([tests/graphics/Readme.md](../../tests/graphics/Readme.md#direct3d-on-venus)), and `matched` already says so; `run.py` now makes the failure check only for a unit with no stop. The reference stays as it is.
