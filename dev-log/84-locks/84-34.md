# 84-34: WebGL's 793 deqp/ pages on Debian and on Rach2, both browsers

Task 84-34 (#4709) runs once, beside Linux, the WebGL conformance suite's 793 `deqp/` pages, which the release leaves out of the Lenovo's WebGL cases and Linux's reference there (`tests/graphics/runners/webgl-lenovo.omit`, by the chat's decision of 2026-11-12, 83-15). They are dEQP's GLES 3 cases ported to WebGL; the native dEQP-GLES2, GLES3 and GLES31 cases run the same cases on the same GPU and driver. The manager split the task: its QEMU half first, at the milestone head, so that the Lenovo never waits on code; then the Lenovo, Debian and Rach2, both browsers.

## The pages

The 793 pages are pages 888 to 1,680 of the 2,865 in WebGL 2.0.1's list, in chunks 3 to 6 of 250 pages: 112 in chunk 3, 250 in each of chunks 4 and 5, and 181 in chunk 6. WebGL 1.0.4's list has none of them. A run of chunks 3 to 6 runs them all and 207 other pages beside them, which the Lenovo's cases already judge.

## Under virgl on the testing PC

The virgl suite runs these chunks in both browsers in every release, judged page by page against the Debian guest's run in the same QEMU (`tests/graphics/ref/virgl-wsl`). Its lists leave out only Chromium's 29 pages of `deqp/functional/gles3/transformfeedback/`, after which the host's GPU engine is reset some 75 pages later in Debian's guest as in Rach2's (`webgl-virgl.omit`). The cases of chunks 3 to 6 ran at the milestone head, eb4abdbcc, on 2026-11-16 from 01:47 to 02:22 UTC+6 on the testing PC's WSL, under KVM, four guests at a time:

    ARCH=x86_64 RACH2_QEMU_ACCEL=kvm RACH2_CASES='machine.virgl.webgl-chromium-webgl2-03 ...
        machine.virgl.webgl-firefox-webgl2-06' tests/run virgl

All eight passed: each ran its whole chunk, and `judge.py webgl` found no page worse than Debian's. In pages, the verdict of each page's second run when the first did not pass, beside the Debian guest's reference:

* Every failing page fails in Debian's guest too, for the causes the reference's Readme gives: in chunk 3, the framebuffer and fragment output pages after virglrenderer refuses Chromium's context on `fbocolorbuffer/tex3d_01.html`; in chunk 4, the `texturefiltering/` pages' image verification and 15 `shadertexturefunction/` pages; in Firefox's chunk 6, 23 of the 29 transform feedback pages that Chromium leaves out, which Firefox runs to the end in both guests. No Rach2 fault shows, and nothing here needed a fix.
* Firefox's chunk 4 passed 42 more pages than the reference, all `texturefiltering/` pages, which pass or fail with how many pages ran before them in the same browser (`webgl-virgl.flakes`, 84-265t).
* Chromium's chunk 3 passed 226,654,172 subtests against Debian's 226,654,171, and chunk 4 463,699,119 against 463,732,567: the counts of `texturefiltering/` pages that fail move from run to run, and the judge leaves those failures out by `webgl-virgl.flakes`.

The 29 transform feedback pages are judged only on the Lenovo, where nothing ends the GPU.

## On the Lenovo

The Debian half needs only the Lenovo; the Rach2 half runs on the installed release once the stack's release has passed, as the card says, and is judged against the Debian half. Chromium's chunks 3 and 4 ran in Debian in 83-15's boot, before the deqp/ pages were cut, every one of their 362 deqp/ pages a pass, in [webgl-chromium-measured.pages](../83-testbed/83-15/webgl-chromium-measured.pages); they count, so Debian runs Chromium's chunks 5 and 6 and Firefox's 3 to 6.

`tools/lenovo/debian-ref/conformance.sh deqp` runs those chunks through `lenovo.sh webgl` with `-` for the reference, without `webgl-lenovo.omit`, and writes each browser's lines to `deqp/BROWSER.pages`. The Rach2 half runs `lenovo.sh webgl BROWSER CHUNK REF` from a stage with no `webgl-lenovo.omit` in it, for chunks 3 to 6 of each browser.

The step, its commands and their times are on #4709. The results go below once it has run.
