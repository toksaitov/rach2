# 84-5779: texturesize.html in machine.virgl.webgl-firefox-webgl2-04

`machine.virgl.webgl-firefox-webgl2-04` failed on x86_64 in three full runs, on 17, 21 and 24 November, each time with one page worse than Debian's: `deqp/functional/gles3/shadertexturefunction/texturesize.html`, page 1146, passed 823 subtests where Firefox's reference passes 824. No single subtest makes the difference. Where the case runs the page, 13 of its checks, on 2D array and 3D textures, fail in most runs and pass in a few, in the Debian guest and on Rach2 alike, so the page's count moves between 823 and 826 on both; 823 is the least either gave in 32 runs, and the reference's 824 came from a run that passed one of the 13. The reference now holds 823, and the case judges the page's other 1,888 subtests as before.

## Which subtests

The page's subtests are its cases' checks: for each of 38 sampler types and shader stages, `TextureSizeCase` (`deqp/functional/gles3/es3fShaderTextureFunctionTests.js`) draws once for each of 36 texture sizes and reads one pixel back, and a pixel that is not white fails the check as "Case N Got unexpected texture size". The records cannot name the subtest: `webgl.html` keeps the first 100 failure messages of each run of a page, and the page fails 1,077 or more, so every record lists the same first 100, Cases 0 to 35 of `sampler2d_fixed_vertex` and `sampler2d_fixed_fragment` and 0 to 27 of `sampler2d_float_vertex`, as Debian's runs of 84-265y do. The runs below raised that limit.

The case's verdict on a page that fails is its second run, in a new browser that runs the chunk's failing pages in order. Before page 1146 these are pages 1034, 1095 and 1132 to 1145, 14 of them its neighbours in `shadertexturefunction/`. One of those compiles a shader that virglrenderer writes with `GL_EXT_texture_shadow_lod`, which the host's driver lacks; QEMU then logs "Dropping rendering due to missing shaders" for the browser's virgl context ([77-vgpu upstream](../77-vgpu/upstream.md#virglrenderer-asks-for-gl_ext_texture_shadow_lod-for-depth-lookups-that-core-glsl-has)). Run alone in a new browser, the page passes every one of its 1,901 subtests in the Debian guest, in 4 runs of 4 (`84-5779/debian/alone-results`).

## The runs

Each leg ran the second run's pages, 1034, 1095, 1132 to 1146 and 1147, in one Firefox, and then each page that failed once more in a new browser, as the case does, eight times over; each run of page 1146 is one count. Both legs ran from 04:22 UTC on 24 November, side by side, each under a ledger claim of 3 processors, under KVM on the testing PC, NVIDIA's driver 621.24, at the milestone branch's c4bac91554:

    sh tests/graphics/runners/debian.sh webgl -p virgl -c 3 OUT -R 8 \
        -P 1034,1095,1132,...,1146,1147 -g -b firefox -v 2.0.1 -k 1 \
        -x /tmp/runners/webgl-virgl.omit 1146
    ARCH=x86_64 RACH2_QEMU_ACCEL=kvm RACH2_CASES=machine.virgl.webgl-firefox-webgl2-04 tests/run virgl

The second with the case's runner command changed to the same `webgl.sh` line and its processors to 3. `-R` and `-P`, the failure limit of 5,000 and a `keepalive` fetch only for an event under 60,000 bytes, since a browser refuses a larger one and the page's result was lost without it, are a diagnostic copy's, not committed (`84-5779/diagnosis.diff`).

In all 32 runs, 1,065 checks failed. Each of 13 others failed in all but one run: `sampler2darray_fixed_vertex` Cases 5 and 10, `isampler2darray_fragment` Case 17 and `usampler2darray_fragment` Cases 29, 33 and 34 passed once each in Debian, and `usampler2darray_vertex` Cases 7 and 19, `usampler3d_fragment` Cases 7 and 8 and `usampler3d_vertex` Cases 18, 20 and 32 once each on Rach2 (`84-5779/tally`). They passed in four runs on each side, one to three neighbouring checks of one case at a time; which checks a run passes is not the same twice, and four such runs a side do not show a difference between the two. With all 13 failed the page counts 823, its count in 12 runs of 16 on each side. The 17 other pages gave one count each, the same on both sides, in every repetition, as the 16 before page 1146 did in five runs of Rach2 and six of Debian before page 1147 was added.

The full runs agree: Rach2's three failed with 823 and its rerun of 21 November passed, Debian's reference under NVIDIA's 621.01 holds 823, and its two chunk runs of 84-265y under 621.24 gave 824.

## What changed

`tests/graphics/ref/virgl-wsl/webgl-firefox-2.0.1/firefox-2.0.1-04.pages` holds 823 passed and 1,078 failed for page 1146, and the reference's Readme says so. The judge passes the page at 823 or more; a fault that failed any of its other checks would count fewer. No kernel code changed: Rach2's count never fell below Debian's least, and the checks that move do so as often on Debian. Why a check passes in a few runs, in a context whose draws virglrenderer drops, was not traced; it is recorded in [upstream faults](upstream.md#webgls-texturesize-page-on-virgl-over-mesas-d3d12-driver-passes-a-few-of-its-checks-in-some-runs-after-a-refused-shader).
