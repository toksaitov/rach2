# 84-32b: the faults of 84-32 that the stack's review found

Task 84-32b (#4704) fixes four faults that Codex's stack-end review found in 84-32's code, now that the DRM rows run without the big lock. Linux references are to 6.12.115. No upstream fault was found.

* The shared stub fence (`dma_fence_get_stub`, `rach2/sys/dmabuf/fence.c`) was made at its first use with no lock, so two first SYNCOBJ_CREATE or SYNCOBJ_SIGNAL calls on two processors could both make it; the second `dma_fence_init` cleared the references the first had taken, and a later put could reach zero and free the static record. It is now made and signalled under a lock of its own, `dma_fence_stub`, and a caller takes its reference only after that lock is let go, as Linux's `dma_fence_get_stub` under `dma_fence_stub_lock` (`drivers/dma-buf/dma-fence.c:27-28` and `131-148`). The stub's own lock is taken once under it, at the signal.
* A fence's deadline hint was compared and stored with no lock, so two waits with WAIT_DEADLINE could lose the earlier one, and on i686 the 64-bit value was read and written as two words. `dma_fence_set_deadline` now compares and stores it under the fence's lock and lets the lock go before it visits the children of an array or a chain, and `dma_fence_deadline`, which SW_SYNC_GET_DEADLINE uses, reads it under the lock, as Linux's `timeline_fence_set_deadline` and `sw_sync_ioctl_get_deadline` (`drivers/dma-buf/sw_sync.c:195-209` and `452-458`); Linux's array and chain hooks visit their children under no lock (`dma-fence-array.c:152-160`, `dma-fence-chain.c:210-218`).
* The games' native cases raise the GPU minimum to 1000 MHz, and `tests/system/games/lenovo.sh` kept the original only in a shell variable, put back by its exit trap, which the native owner's SIGKILL of a forced stop skips. etude-guest now has a fixture `gpumin`, after 83-17b's `private`: before the case it records each card's `gt_min_freq_mhz` in the attempt's record of its fixtures, outside the workspace, and once every process of the case has ended it writes each back, from `fixture_cleanup`, or from `fixture_settle` when etude-guest ended before the case. `games/supertuxkart` and `games/0ad` take it. `lenovo.sh` keeps its own trap for a run by hand.
* i915's Readme said that its buffer rows still took the big lock. Its Locks section now says that every row is `DRM_UNLOCKED`, as every row of Linux's table (`drivers/gpu/drm/i915/i915_driver.c:1688-1747`), and which locks each row takes.

## Build

Both kernels linked with `make -j8 ARCH=i686 rach2` and `make -j8 ARCH=x86_64 rach2`, and `check-kernel` passed each; both makes then stopped at the tool preflight, which refused the testing PC's disk, 33 GiB free against the 40 GiB a full build needs. etude-guest compiled with both cross compilers, and the host's `etude`, `tests/native/attempt.c` and the host's `fixture.c` compiled with no warning. No test or boot ran; the proof is Codex's review of the fix and the release, whose native games cases run with the fixture.
