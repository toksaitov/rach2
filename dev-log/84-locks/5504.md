# 5504: wait4 and waitid without the big lock

5496 measured a traced call's stops under the big lock; strace's `wait4`, two a stop, took the lock at each call ([5496](5496.md)). This task runs `wait4`, `waitid` and i686's `waitpid` without it, as Linux 6.12's `do_wait` runs under `tasklist_lock` and the child's `siglock`.

## The change

* The look at the caller's children and tracees walks the indexes by parent and by tracer under the list lock as a reader, the lookup task's `tasklist_lock`, and reads and reserves each child's events under `sig_lock` (`wait_scan`, `wait_lock` in `rach2/sys/syscall_proc.c`). The list lock is one spinlock, so two threads' looks do not overlap.
* A tracee publishes its ptrace stop, the status and its unreported mark together, under `sig_lock` (`signal_trap_publish`, called by `ptrace_stop`), so a look reads them as one. A stop or a continue of a process, which `sig_group_stop` writes under the big lock alone, is reserved by a compare-and-exchange that finds the value the look read. A reserved stop or continue holds a reference to its record, so a report given back after a failed copy finds the record it reserved.
* An end, a zombie's reap, a traced thread's free or a tracer's report of a process's end, is taken under the big lock: its release sleeps and frees what that lock guards, and the ending thread holds the lock from the zombie's publication until it has left the processor. A look without the lock that meets one takes the lock and looks again, so the reports keep the walk's order.
* The ids of a PID or user namespace below the first are translated under the big lock (`ids_lock`), and waitid looks a pidfd up under it.
* The sleep is unchanged: the group's `chld` queue, which an end, a stop and a continue wake, joined before the look.

## Measured in QEMU

The x86-64 lockdep kernel at 331cacc91 ran 5496's bench, with four processors under KVM on the testing PC, on 2026-11-19 from 23:03 UTC+6; the output and the inputs' SHA-256s are in [5504/lockdep.txt](5504/lockdep.txt). Every run ended with status 0, the bench reached `BENCH DONE`, and no line of the log names lockdep.

The bench's profiled `strace -f` run of 20000 getppid calls zeroes and reads `bkl/calls`. Its line for `wait4`, number 61:

In the first namespaces a wait takes the big lock only after its look has met an end (`wait_find`), and the profiled run has two ends, strace's traced child, which strace reaps, and strace, which the bench reaps; the third hold was not traced to its call. `ptrace`, number 101, still takes the lock at each of its 80097 calls, and the traced getppid at each of its 20000, since a traced thread's call runs under it; those are the ptrace and signal-posting tasks' (84-5496b, 84-5496c). The lockdep kernel's times are not compared with 5496's, which ran the kernel without the checker.
