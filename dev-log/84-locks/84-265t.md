# 84-265t: the texture filtering pages of machine.virgl.webgl-chromium-webgl2-04

`machine.virgl.webgl-chromium-webgl2-04` failed on x86_64 twice, in the release's rerun with 12 pages worse than Debian's and in loop a's run at the branch head with 13. Every one of them is a dEQP texture filtering page that fails each of its cases in Debian's stored reference too, and differs from it only in its count of subtests passed. A new run of the same chunk in the Debian guest fails against its own reference on 10 of the same pages, with Rach2's counts. Rach2 behaves as Linux does; the pages are named as flakes in `tests/graphics/runners/webgl-virgl.flakes`, and the case stays judged on its other pages.

## What the count measures

Every case of the chunk's 103 pages under `deqp/functional/gles3/texturefiltering/` fails with "Image verification failed" in the reference and in both Rach2 runs; the failed subtests are the same in all three, 6, 9 or 18 a page. What differs is the count of passed subtests, which runs to millions on a page of 9 cases.

The suite's harness counts each `assertMsgOptions` that holds as a passed subtest. The texture verifier (`computeTextureLookupDiff3D` and its 2D-array twin in `deqp/modules/shared/glsTextureTestUtil.js`) first compares each pixel with the ideal image, and only a pixel that misses it goes to the slow lookup verifier (`deqp/framework/common/tcuTexLookupVerifier.js`), whose assertions are the ones counted. A case stops at its first failing iteration. So the count is the number of checks of pixels that missed the ideal image, up to that iteration: more pixels off the ideal image give a higher count. A page that passes outright counts 1,027.

## What is left judged

The flake line names the message "Image verification failed" on every page of the directory, so such a page counts as passing when that is its only failure; a case that fails any other way, times out or is not run is still worse. The texture filtering itself stays judged case by case in the `machine.virgl.deqp-gles3-*` cases, whose list holds 1,124 cases of `dEQP-GLES3.functional.texture.filtering` and whose reference passes 272 of its 324 3D combinations.

Why the results move with the run and its history, in a stack of the guest's Mesa, virglrenderer, Mesa's D3D12 driver and NVIDIA's driver, was not traced; it is recorded in [upstream faults](upstream.md#webgls-texture-filtering-pages-on-virgl-over-mesas-d3d12-driver-change-with-the-run-and-the-pages-before-them).
