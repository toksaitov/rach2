# The msr device, i915 runtime PM and i2c-dev

Task 5235 takes the Lenovo's skips of `kms_pm_rpm`, 28 results in the native `gpu/igt-kms.8` at f8104921a, and of `kms_vblank`'s `ts-continuation-dpms-rpm` and `ts-continuation-modeset-rpm`, 6 results in `gpu/igt-kms.9`, all of which Linux's reference passes. The runs' own output is in the main clone's `build/tmp/lenovo-igt/5380f1ecd49300cf` and `build/tmp/lenovo-igt/229151b5047349ac`.

## The cause

Every `kms_pm_rpm` subtest skipped in `setup_pc8` (`tests/intel/kms_pm_rpm.c:697`) on `modprobe("msr") == 0`: IGT reads package C8 residency from the processor's MSRs through `/dev/cpu/N/msr`, which Rach2 did not have. Past that requirement, both tests call `igt_setup_runtime_pm`, which writes the PCI function's `power/control` and `power/autosuspend_delay_ms` and reads debugfs's `i915_runtime_pm_status`; Rach2 had no runtime power management, so the device never slept and IGT's wait for `suspended` could not end. `kms_pm_rpm@i2c` also needs Linux's i2c-dev: `/dev/i2c-N` for each of the display's buses, the connector's `ddc` link to its adapter, and `modprobe i2c-dev` succeeding.

## The fix

* `/dev/cpu/N/msr` (`rach2/sys/arch/x86/msrdev.c`), as Linux's `arch/x86/kernel/msr.c`: root reads and writes 8 bytes at the register's index, on that processor, a #GP answered with `EIO`; its class device and `/sys/module/msr/initstate` reading `live`, so libkmod's modprobe succeeds. The i686 kernel gained the optional RDMSR and WRMSR helpers x86-64 has, and `/dev` rows of their own for `/dev/cpu/N`.
* A runtime PM core (`rach2/sys/pm_runtime.c`) after Linux's: holds, an autosuspend delay, a driver's prepare, suspend and resume, and the device's `power` directory. The DRM core holds the device across ioctls, commit tails and connector probes through new `pm_get` and `pm_put` hooks. The PCI layer reads and sets a function's power state between D0 and D3hot.
* i915's runtime PM (`i915_runtime_pm.c`), as Linux 6.12's `intel_runtime_suspend` and `intel_runtime_resume`: the device sleeps after 10 s idle with GTT maps revoked, every interrupt bank masked and its handlers finished, package C8 allowed as `hsw_enable_pc8` (the PCH's partition gating, CLKOUT_DP off over the sideband, the CD clock on FCLK, LCPLL off, D_COMP's compensation off through the pcode, LCPLL let power down), and D3hot; every register access while asleep is refused and logged. The wake undoes each step, `hsw_disable_pc8` under forcewake. Where Linux's `assert_can_disable_lcpll` only warns, Rach2 refuses the sleep and logs the reason, so that a missing hold leaves the device awake.
* An I2C core with i2c-dev (`rach2/sys/i2c.c`): `/dev/i2c-N`, reads, writes, `I2C_RDWR`, Linux's SMBus emulation with PEC, the i386 translation, and `/sys/module/i2c_dev` live; i915 registers each GMBUS pin and each DisplayPort channel as an adapter with Linux's names (`intel_i2c_dev.c`) and gives each connector its `ddc` link.
* The 34 rows again require Linux's passes in `tests/graphics/ref/lenovo/expect/igt-kms.rows`.

Faults the work met and fixed: the MMIO and fake chip inits left the new `asleep` flag as stack bytes, which refused every register access at the bind (e861437e6); the topology read of `i915_sseu_init` at the first context touched the registers outside any hold (77dc4a211); a runtime PM change made with no current process matched a get from such a context as its own, which then skipped the resume (53983f3db); and the sideband's waits were bounded by 100 us where Linux's `intel_sbi_rw` allows 100 ms (89c968f6b). The last two came from comparing this work with task 5261's `i915_pc8.c`, as the manager asked; the rest of that branch's PC8 and LCPLL sequences match this one's.

## Proof

`tests/host/i915_rpm_hosttest.c`, run alone, runs `rpm_prepare`, `rpm_suspend` and `rpm_resume` on the HD 4400's model, whose new `i915_model_pc8.c` locks and unlocks LCPLL and answers the sideband, with the pcode writing D_COMP. It checks hsw_enable_pc8's writes in Linux's order after every bank is masked, every access refused afterwards, hsw_disable_pc8's under forcewake and each bank restored, twice; each refusal before any write; and a hung sideband, LCPLL and pcode whose waits end at their bounds, 427 ms in all. It does not reach D3hot, since the model is not the MMIO backend.

Fake-chip boots in QEMU on both architectures (`-device edu`, `rach2.i915fake`) showed six `/dev/i2c-N`, `I2C_FUNCS` 0xeff0009, the device suspended 500 ms after its last use with the autosuspend delay at 500, woken by an ioctl or `control` set to `on`, and no access while asleep; the fake chip's unmodelled waits end at their bounds.
