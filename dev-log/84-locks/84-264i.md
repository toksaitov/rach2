# 84-264i: virtio-blk

84-264e predicted that a virtio-blk disk would remove the IDE exits of the disk cases, 879,433 in `ext2` and 3.2 million in `diskrate`, since virtio's notifications go to KVM's ioeventfds and never reach QEMU. Under KVM the same workload on the same disk made 4,419,940 accesses to the IDE controller on IDE and 194 to the virtio-blk device on virtio, for nearly the same 14,700 disk commands.

## Measurement

One i686 kernel booted a copy of the same 64 MiB KRFFS disk with `rach2.fsdisk` under KVM in WSL on the testing PC, with 4 processors and 512 MiB, once as the PC's IDE primary master and once as a `virtio-blk-pci` device. Each boot logged in at the serial console, wrote 16 MiB from `/dev/urandom` to a file, synced, hashed it twice, removed it and synced, read `/bin/busybox` four times, printed `/proc/disk` and powered off. QEMU traced `memory_region_ops_read` and `_write`, which [count.awk](84-264e/count.awk) counted by region, as in 84-264e.

| Bus | Disk commands | Flushes | IDE ports | IDE bus-master DMA | virtio-blk | All traced |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IDE | 14,756 | 144 | 4,318,668 | 101,272 | | 4,878,317 |
| virtio | 14,706 | 234 | 546 | 0 | 194 | 467,531 |

On IDE a command costs about 300 accesses: the task file, status and alternate-status reads and the bus-master registers. On virtio the 194 accesses are the device's bring-up: its common configuration, a byte-wise read of its device configuration and one read of its interrupt status. The 546 IDE accesses are the ATA driver's probe of the empty channels that the PC machine keeps. The rest of both runs is mostly the mouse probe's i8042 reads, 84-264k's, and the VGA memory and serial console beside them.

## Cases

The disks that only carry a system moved to virtio-blk: the GNU root's machines and their data disks, and every Rach2 machine of `ext2`. The cases that test disks, partitions, installs, the read-only policy and AHCI itself stayed on AHCI, and the image helpers stayed on IDE. `machine.diskrate.storage` stayed on IDE as well, since its rows judge the ATA driver's bus-master DMA and its status reads a command; its 3.2 million exits are the price of that driver's only cover in QEMU.
